# 05. 代名詞與主詞動詞一致

> 來源：[筆記平台 · 英文](https://hhtim.github.io/notes-platform/english/#/lesson/grammar-pronouns-agreement)　第 5 課 / 共 21 課 · 約 42 分鐘

**看空格位置選主格、受格、所有格或反身代名詞，everyone 與 each other 的用法，再練找出真正的主詞，以及 and、neither…nor、金額時間、some of、the number of、there is 這些特殊主詞；53 題課中練習。**

### 讀完這課你會

*   判斷空格要填主格、受格還是所有格
*   用對 everyone、each other 這類代名詞
*   找出真正的主詞，不被中間的片語騙走
*   處理 A and B、either…or 這類特殊主詞

*【同一個人，五種樣子】*

## 人稱代名詞：主格、受格、所有格

**代名詞**（pronoun）是代替名詞的字，前面已經講過的人或東西，再提到時就不必重複名詞。同一個人依照在句子裡的位置，有五種樣子：

| 主格<br>（當主詞） | 受格<br>（當受詞） | 所有格形容詞<br>（後面接名詞） | 所有格代名詞<br>（自己代替名詞） | 反身代名詞<br>（指回主詞） |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `I` | `me` | `my` | `mine` | `myself` |
| `you` | `you` | `your` | `yours` | `yourself`／`yourselves` |
| `he` | `him` | `his` | `his` | `himself` |
| `she` | `her` | `her` | `hers` | `herself` |
| `it` | `it` | `its` | （不用） | `itself` |
| `we` | `us` | `our` | `ours` | `ourselves` |
| `they` | `them` | `their` | `theirs` | `themselves` |

its 不當所有格代名詞用（不說 This book is its），只在 its own 裡出現。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-possessive-my-mine-your-yours-etc) 對照表：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/possessives-pronouns)、[劍橋（反身代名詞）](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-reflexive-myself-themselves-etc)

#### 看位置決定用哪一格

| 空格的位置 | 填 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 動詞前面，當主詞 | 主格 | `She will present the results.` |
| 動詞後面當受詞；介系詞（to、for、with）後面 | 受格 | `Please call me.`、`Send it to them.` |
| 名詞前面 | 所有格形容詞 | `their new office` |
| 自己代替「所有格＋名詞」，後面沒有名詞 | 所有格代名詞 | `This desk is mine.`（＝my desk） |
| 受詞就是主詞自己；或強調「親自」 | 反身代名詞 | `He introduced himself.` |

*   **介系詞後面**也用受格：`with me`，不說 `with I`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/personal-pronouns)
*   **所有格代名詞不放在名詞前面**：說 `our friends`，不說 `ours friends`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-possessive-my-mine-your-yours-etc)
*   所有格形容詞（my、your 這類）有些書叫 possessive determiner，指的是同一組字。

#### 幾個容易錯的地方

*   **「我和同事」當主詞用 I**：口語有時會聽到 `My friends and me went…`，考試與書面用 `My friends and I went…`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-personal-i-me-you-him-it-they-etc) 在受詞位置就用受格：`The manager asked my colleague and me to join.`。判斷方法：把「我的同事 and」遮住，看剩下的 I 還是 me 通。
*   **所有格代名詞不加撇號：**✗ `your's`、`her's`、`their's` → ✓ `yours`、`hers`、`theirs`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/possessives-pronouns)
*   **its 對 it's**：所有格形容詞 `its` 沒有撇號；`it's` 一定是 it is 或 it has。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/possessives-adjectives)
*   **a friend of mine：**✗ `a friend of me` → ✓ `Susan is a friend of mine.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/possessives-pronouns)
*   **不知道是男是女，用 they／their**：each 常搭配複數代名詞與所有格：`Each member of the community should take pride in their local environment.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/each)

例句：

*   `Mr. Huang called us yesterday. He wants to change the order.`（黃先生昨天打電話給我們，他想改訂單。）
*   `Please send the invoice to her by Friday.`（請在週五前把發票寄給她。）
*   `The clients were happy with their new contract.`（客戶對新合約很滿意。）
*   `This laptop isn't mine. I think it's yours.`（這台筆電不是我的，應該是你的。）
*   `My manager and I will attend the trade fair.`（我和主管會去參加商展。）
*   `The company changed its logo last year.`（公司去年換了標誌。）

*【指回自己，或強調親自】*

## 反身代名詞：-self、-selves

反身代名詞（reflexive pronoun）是 -self／-selves 結尾、指回主詞的代名詞。三種用法：

*   **受詞就是主詞自己**：受詞跟主詞是同一個人時，受詞用反身代名詞；常見的動詞有 `introduce`、`prepare`、`blame`、`enjoy`、`help`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reflexive-pronouns)

    *   `Agnes looked at herself in the mirror.`（看自己）對 `Agnes looked at her in the mirror.`（看另一個人）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-reflexive-myself-themselves-etc)
    *   請客人自己拿吃的喝的：`Would you like to help yourself to another drink?`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reflexive-pronouns)

*   **強調「親自、本人」：**`The director of the company wrote to us himself to apologise for the dreadful service.`（公司總經理親自寫信向我們道歉。）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-reflexive-myself-themselves-etc) 這種強調用法常放在子句最後。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reflexive-pronouns)

*   **by oneself＝獨自、沒人幫忙**：反身代名詞常和 by 連用，例如 `by herself`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-reflexive-myself-themselves-etc)

兩個不能用的地方：

*   **不能單獨當主詞：**✗ `my manager and myself` → ✓ `My manager and I`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-reflexive-myself-themselves-etc)
*   **表示位置的介系詞後面用受格：**`He had a suitcase beside him.`，不用 beside himself。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reflexive-pronouns)

例句：

*   `Before the interview, Mr. Ota introduced himself to the panel.`（面試前，太田先生先向面試官自我介紹。）
*   `Ms. Chen designed the whole website by herself.`（陳小姐自己一個人做完整個網站。）
*   `The CEO himself answered our questions.`（執行長親自回答我們的問題。）
*   `Please help yourselves to coffee in the break room.`（茶水間的咖啡請自行取用。）
*   `The interns organized the welcome party themselves.`（實習生自己辦了迎新會。）

*【不特定的人，與彼此】*

## everyone、someone、nobody，one／ones，each other

#### 不定代名詞接單數動詞

**不定代名詞**（indefinite pronoun）是不指明是誰、是什麼的代名詞：`everyone`／`everybody`／`everything`、`someone`／`somebody`／`something`、`anyone`／`anybody`／`anything`、`no one`／`nobody`／`nothing`。這些字都接單數動詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/pronouns-indefinite-body-one-thing-where)[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/indefinite-pronouns)

> Everyone is、Someone has、Nobody knows：意思是很多人，動詞仍然用單數。

*   **指回去時通常用 they／their：**`Everybody enjoyed the concert. They stood up and clapped.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/indefinite-pronouns) 所以是 `Everyone has their own password.`：動詞單數，指回去的所有格用 their。
*   **nobody、no one、nothing 不再加否定：**✗ `Nobody didn't come.` → ✓ `Nobody came.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/indefinite-pronouns)
*   **加 's 變所有格：**`someone's umbrella`、`somebody else's coat`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/indefinite-pronouns)

#### one／ones：代替前面的可數名詞

one 和複數 ones 常用來代替前面的可數名詞；不可數名詞不用 one 指回去。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/one)

*   `This printer is too slow. We need a faster one.`
*   `The old chairs are fine; we don't need new ones.`

#### each other／one another：彼此

each other 和 one another 表示一群（兩個以上）人裡，每個人都對其他人做某件事。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/each-other-one-another)

*   each other 和 one another 差別很小。
*   沒有複數：✗ `each others`。
*   each other 不當子句的主詞。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reciprocal-pronouns)
*   跟 themselves 意思不同：`talk to each other` 是你跟我說、我跟你說，`talk to themselves` 是各自自言自語。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reciprocal-pronouns)

例句：

*   `Everyone in the department is invited to the party.`（部門所有人都受邀參加派對。）
*   `Someone from IT is coming to fix the printer.`（IT 部門會有人來修印表機。）
*   `If anyone has questions, they can email me.`（有問題的人可以寄信給我。）
*   `Nobody knew the answer.`（沒有人知道答案。）
*   `We need a bigger meeting room; this one is too small.`（我們需要大一點的會議室，這間太小。）
*   `The two teams help each other during busy periods.`（兩個小組在忙季互相支援。）

*【先找主詞，再決定動詞】*

## 主詞動詞一致：別被中間的字騙了

主詞的人稱與單複數，決定動詞的人稱與單複數。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/subject-verb-agreement) 規則很簡單，難的是找主詞：主詞後面常常夾著一大串修飾，離動詞最近的名詞不一定是主詞。動詞要跟主詞的中心名詞一致：[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/agreement-with-the-right-noun-phrase-1/)

*   `I know that my mother, who has four siblings, loves me.`：離 loves 最近的是 siblings，但主詞是 mother。

> 先把修飾主詞的那一串括起來，剩下的主詞是單數還是複數，動詞就跟它。

常見的三種「夾在中間」：

| 夾在中間的東西 | 例句（括號裡的是修飾，不影響動詞） |
| --- | --- |
| 介系詞片語（of、in、for、from…） | `The list (of approved suppliers) is on the shared drive.` |
| as well as、along with（意思像 and，文法上不算） | `The director, (as well as her assistants,) is attending.` |
| 關係子句（who、which、that 開頭） | `The engineer (who designed these machines) is retiring.` |

along with、as well as、besides 開頭的片語夾在主詞和動詞之間時，決定單複數先忽略它們：`The politician, along with the journalist, is expected tomorrow.`[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/subjects-containing-along-with-as-well-as-and-besides-1/)

#### 一句話有兩個動詞時：各跟各的主詞

句子裡有關係子句時，就有兩組主詞與動詞，每個動詞跟它自己那個子句的主詞一致。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/agreement-with-the-right-noun-phrase-1/) 以 `The engineer who designed these machines is retiring.` 為例：

*   `is retiring` 的主詞是 engineer。
*   關係子句裡 `designed` 的主詞是 who，who 指的就是前面的 engineer。

所以關係子句裡的動詞，要看 who／which／that 指的是哪個名詞：

*   `Applicants who submit…`（applicants 是複數）
*   `An applicant who submits…`（單數）

關係子句的完整用法在〈關係詞〉那課。

#### one of＋複數名詞：動詞跟 one

one of 後面的名詞用複數，但主詞是 one，動詞用單數：`One of my friends is in hospital.`（friends 是複數，動詞是 is）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/countability-one-of)

例句：

*   `The results of the customer survey are on the shared drive.`（顧客問卷的結果放在共用硬碟。）主詞 results。
*   `The cost of repairs to the delivery vans has increased.`（貨車的維修費漲了。）主詞 cost。
*   `The proposal, along with the budget figures, was sent to the board.`（提案連同預算數字一起送到董事會。）主詞 proposal。
*   `One of our suppliers has raised its prices.`（我們有一家供應商漲價了。）主詞 one。
*   `Employees who work night shifts receive extra pay.`（上夜班的員工有加給。）who 指 employees，所以 work；主要子句的動詞 receive 也跟 employees。

*【特殊主詞，一條一條記】*

## and、either…or、金額時間、some of、there is

| 主詞長這樣 | 動詞 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A and B | 複數 | `The sales manager and the designer are meeting the client.` |
| both A and B | 複數 | `Both the printer and the scanner need repairs.` |
| each／every＋單數名詞 | 單數 | `Every desk has a lamp.`、`Each report includes a summary.` |
| A or B、either A or B、neither A nor B（兩個都單數） | 正式寫法用單數 | `Either Mr. Lee or Ms. Wu is available.` |
| 同上，一單一複 | 跟靠近動詞的那個（傳統規則） | `Neither the manager nor the assistants are available.` |
| 金額、一段時間 | 單數（當成一筆、一段） | `Ten thousand dollars is too much.` |
| some／most／half／all of＋名詞 | 看 of 後面的名詞 | `Most of the information is accurate.`、`Half of the employees are here.` |
| a number of＋複數名詞 | 複數（＝很多） | `A number of clients have called.` |
| the number of＋複數名詞，談數字本身 | 單數（談它的多少、漲跌） | `The number of complaints has fallen.` |
| 不可數名詞、news | 單數 | `The news is good.` |
| There is／There are | 看 be 後面的名詞 | `There is a problem.`、`There are two problems.` |

表裡每一列的細節：

*   **and、both**：用 and 連起來的幾個主詞，動詞用複數。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/noun-phrases-noun-phrases-and-verbs) both 帶的名詞接複數動詞，either、neither 帶的名詞接單數動詞。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/quantifiers)

*   **each、every**：every 當主詞用單數動詞[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/every)；each、everyone、anyone、someone 也都是單數，接單數動詞。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/indefinite-pronouns-and-agreement-1/)

    *   `each of the＋複數名詞` 當主詞，通常接單數動詞，口語也會聽到複數。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/each)
    *   `neither of the＋複數名詞` 也一樣：正式寫法用單數，口語常用複數。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/neither-neither-nor-and-not-either)
    *   正式的書面英文用單數比較保險。

*   **or、either…or、neither…nor**：用 or 連接時，單數名詞配單數動詞，複數名詞配複數動詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/or)

    *   兩個單數用 either…or、neither…nor 連起來：正式英文一般用單數，比較不正式時也會用複數。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/singular-noun-phrases-connected-by-either/or-1/)
    *   一單一複：動詞跟最靠近的那個名詞；建議把複數那個放在最靠近動詞的位置，用複數動詞。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/connected-singular-and-plural-noun-phrases-1/)
    *   「跟最近的名詞」是傳統規則，實際用法差異很大。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/quantifiers)

*   **金額、時間**：金額或一段時間用單數動詞：`Ten dollars is a high price to pay.`、`Five years is the maximum sentence for that offence.`[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/sums-of-money-and-periods-of-time-1/)

*   **some of、most of、half of、percent of**：這類表示「一部分」的字，看 of 後面的名詞決定單複數。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/words-that-indicate-portions-1/) 例如 `Most of the information was useful.`（information 不可數，用單數）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/most)

*   **不可數名詞**：在主詞動詞一致上一律當單數。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/uncountable-nouns-1/) a number of、the number of、news、集合名詞（staff、team、the police）的完整說明在〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉那課。

*   **there is／there are**：there is 和 there's 都是單數形，there are 是複數形。口語裡有人用 there's 接複數，但正式寫作或考試裡可能被認為不正確。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/there-is-there-are) 所以動詞要往後看，看 be 後面的名詞是單數還是複數。

#### 解題順序

*   **第一步**：選項是 is／are、has／have 這種只差單複數的，就是在考主詞動詞一致。
*   **第二步**：把主詞後面的修飾括起來（of 片語、as well as、who／which 子句），找出真正的主詞。
*   **第三步**：主詞是不是上表的特殊主詞？是的話照表；不是就看它本身單數或複數。
*   **第四步**：再看時間線索決定時態（時態見〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉以後的幾課）。

### 重點整理

*   看位置選格：動詞前面用主格（I、she），動詞或介系詞後面用受格（me、her），名詞前面用所有格形容詞（my、their），單獨代替名詞用所有格代名詞（mine、theirs），受詞是主詞自己用反身代名詞（myself）。
*   My manager and I（主詞）、to Ms. Tan and me（受詞）：遮住另一個人再判斷。its、theirs、yours 都沒有撇號；a friend of mine。
*   反身代名詞三種用法：受詞是自己（introduce himself）、強調親自（The CEO himself）、by oneself＝獨自；不能自己當主詞，表示位置的介系詞後面用受格（beside her）。
*   everyone、someone、nobody 接單數動詞，指回去用 they／their；nobody 不再加否定；one／ones 代替可數名詞；each other 是「彼此」，沒有 each others。
*   找真正的主詞：of 片語、as well as、along with、who／which 子句都只是修飾；one of＋複數名詞接單數動詞；關係子句裡的動詞跟 who／which 指的名詞。
*   特殊主詞：A and B、both A and B 用複數；each／every＋單數名詞用單數；neither A nor B 一單一複時跟靠近的；金額、一段時間用單數；some／most／half of 看 of 後面；the number of 談數字本身（多少、漲跌）用單數，a number of＝很多用複數；there is／are 看後面的名詞。

**Q1. Ms. Ito asked ______ to prepare the slides for Monday's meeting.**

- (A) me
- (B) I
- (C) my
- (D) mine

**Q2. The interns presented ______ ideas to the board.**

- (A) their
- (B) they
- (C) them
- (D) theirs

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 空格後面接名詞 ideas，要所有格形容詞 their；theirs 不放在名詞前面。

</details>

**Q3. Nobody helped Mr. Park; he repaired the printer by ______.**

- (A) himself
- (B) him
- (C) his
- (D) he

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** by＋反身代名詞＝獨自、沒人幫忙：by himself。

</details>

**Q4. Everyone in the office ______ to update the software by Friday.**

- (A) needs
- (B) need
- (C) needing
- (D) to need

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** everyone 是不定代名詞，接單數動詞 needs；needing、to need 不是有時態的動詞。

</details>

**Q5. The cost of the new machines ______ higher than the cost of the old ones.**

- (A) is
- (B) are
- (C) were
- (D) have been

**答案：(A)** 主詞是 cost（單數），of the new machines 只是修飾，動詞跟 cost 用單數 is。are、were、have been 都是配複數主詞的形式。

- (A) is
- (B) are
- (C) were
- (D) have been

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

</details>

- (B) is
- (C) was
- (D) has been
