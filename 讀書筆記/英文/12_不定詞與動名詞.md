# 12. 不定詞與動名詞

> 來源：[筆記平台 · 英文](https://hhtim.github.io/notes-platform/english/#/lesson/grammar-infinitive-gerund)　第 12 課 / 共 21 課 · 約 48 分鐘

**不定詞與動名詞當主詞、受詞、補語；動詞後面接 to 還是 -ing 的四類整理（suggest、consider、postpone 只接 -ing，remember、stop、try 意思會變），make／let／have 與 ask／allow＋人＋to，look forward to、be used to 這類介系詞 to，以及表目的、too…to、enough to；67 題課中練習。**

### 讀完這課你會

*   說出不定詞與動名詞在句子裡的位置
*   看動詞決定後面接 to 還是 -ing
*   用對 make／let／have＋受詞＋原形
*   認出 to 是介系詞的片語

*【兩種「動詞變成的名詞」】*

## 不定詞與動名詞：當主詞、受詞、補語

〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉講過：to＋原形、-ing 不能當句子的主要動詞。那它們能做什麼？這課的答案是：它們可以把一個動作「打包」成一樣東西，放在名詞的位置上。

*   **不定詞 infinitive**：有兩種形式：帶 to 的是 to＋動詞原形，例如 `to sign`、`to review`；不帶 to 的就是原形，例如 `sign`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/infinitives-with-and-without-to)
*   **動名詞 -ing 當名詞**：動詞的 -ing 形式可以當名詞用，例如 `Swimming is very good for your health.`。這種 -ing 名詞幾乎都是不可數名詞。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/ing-forms)

-ing 名詞可以放在三個位置：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/ing-forms)

*   動詞的主詞：`Learning English is not easy.`
*   動詞的受詞：`We enjoy learning English.`
*   介系詞的受詞：`Some people are not interested in learning English.`

不定詞和動名詞並排比較：

| 位置 | 動名詞（-ing） | 不定詞（to＋原形） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主詞 | `Reviewing contracts takes time.`（審合約很花時間。） | `It takes time to review contracts.`：常用 `it` 先佔住主詞位置（文法書叫**虛主詞**），真正的主詞放後面 |
| 動詞的受詞 | `We finished reviewing the contract.` | `We decided to review the contract.`：接哪一種要看動詞，下一段專講 |
| 介系詞的受詞 | `Thank you for reviewing the contract.` | 不行：介系詞後面不接不定詞 |
| 補語（be 動詞後面） | `One of my tasks is answering customer e-mails.`（我的工作之一是回覆客戶信件。） | `Our priority is to review the contract.`（我們的第一要務是審合約。） |

*   不定詞也可以自己當主詞：`To work in a developing country had always been her ambition.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/infinitives-with-and-without-to)
*   表達看法常用 it＋be＋形容詞＋to：`It's easy to play the piano.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/infinitives)
*   介系詞後面不用不定詞，帶不帶 to 都不行：✗ `useful for clean`、✗ `useful for to clean` → ✓ `useful for cleaning`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/infinitives-with-and-without-to)
*   不定詞當補語的句型是 `sb's priority is to do sth`：`At the moment, our priority is to recruit high-calibre employees to supplement the existing staff.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/priority)
*   plan 也能這樣接 to＋原形：`My plan is to sell the house and buy an apartment.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/plan)
*   -ing 也能當補語：`Angela's hobby is restoring vintage motorcycles.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/hobby)

一個 -ing 片語當主詞時，把它當成一件事，動詞用單數：`Reviewing contracts takes time.`，主詞是「審合約這件事」，不是 contracts。這跟上面「-ing 名詞幾乎都是不可數名詞」是同一件事：不可數名詞當主詞，動詞用單數。

*   `Answering customer e-mails is part of my job.`（回覆客戶信件是我工作的一部分。）-ing 當主詞。
*   `It is important to back up your files every day.`（每天備份檔案很重要。）虛主詞 `it` 先佔住主詞位置。
*   `Our goal is to open two new branches next year.`（我們的目標是明年開兩家分店。）不定詞當補語。
*   `The team is good at solving technical problems.`（這個團隊很會解決技術問題。）介系詞 `at` 後面接 -ing。
*   `Before leaving the office, please turn off the lights.`（離開辦公室前請關燈。）`before` 後面直接接動詞時用 -ing，又如 `I had something to eat before leaving.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)
*   `We enjoyed working with your team.`（我們很高興和貴團隊合作。）-ing 當動詞 `enjoy` 的受詞。

## 動詞後面接 to 還是 -ing：只接 to、只接 -ing、兩種都行

一個動詞後面再接一個動詞時，第二個動詞通常要變成 to＋原形或 -ing。用哪一種沒有道理可講，**由第一個動詞決定**，只能一個一個記。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/verbs-followed-ing-or-infinitive) 多益常見的整理如下：

| 種類 | 動詞 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 只接 to＋原形 | `decide`、`plan`、`agree`、`expect`、`hope`、`offer`、`refuse`、`manage`、`afford`、`promise`、`want`、`choose`、`would like` | `The supplier agreed to lower its prices.` |
| 只接 -ing | `avoid`、`consider`、`finish`、`suggest`、`recommend`、`postpone`、`delay`、`mind`、`deny`、`enjoy`、`admit`、`keep`、`risk`、`give up`、`put off` | `We are considering opening a branch in Kaohsiung.` |
| 兩種都行，意思差不多 | `begin`、`start`、`continue`、`prefer`、`like`、`love`、`hate` | `Prices began to rise.`＝`Prices began rising.` |
| 兩種都行，意思不同 | `remember`、`forget`、`stop`、`try`、`regret` | 下一段專講 |

上表的動詞再列完整一點：

| 種類 | 動詞 |
| --- | --- |
| 後面接動詞時一定用 to＋原形 | `want`、`learn`、`offer`、`afford`、`agree`、`ask`、`choose`、`decide`、`expect`、`hope`、`plan`、`prepare`、`promise`、`refuse`、`would like` |
| 後面接動詞時一定用 -ing | `enjoy`、`admit`、`mind`、`avoid`、`can't help`、`consider`、`dislike`、`feel like`、`finish`、`give up`、`miss`、`practise`、`suggest` |
| 通常接 -ing、不接 to | `deny`、`keep (on)`、`risk`、`put off` |

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/verbs-followed-ing-or-infinitive)、[劍橋（通常接 -ing 那一列）](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-infinitive-or-verb-ing)

*   `manage` 接 to＋原形：`Did you manage to get any bread?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/manage)
*   `recommend` 接 -ing，下一小節有對錯對照。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-recommend)
*   `postpone` 後面的動詞只用 -ing：`We've had to postpone going to France because the children are ill.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/postpone)
*   `delay` 也只用 -ing：`I think we should delay deciding about this until next year.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/delay)
*   `begin` 兩種都可以：`Jane has just begun learning to drive.`、`she was beginning to get angry`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/begin)
*   `continue` 兩種都可以：`the band continued to play`、`He paused for a moment to listen and then continued eating.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/continue)

#### 只接 -ing 這一組最容易錯

中文習慣說「建議去做」「考慮去做」，很容易寫成 suggest to、consider to：

*   **suggest：**✗ `suggest to do something` → ✓ `suggest doing something`。要講誰去做，就用 `suggest (that) you…`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-suggest)
*   **suggest＋人＋to 也不行：**✗ `He suggested me to apply…` → ✓ `He suggested that I should apply…`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/suggest)
*   **recommend：**✗ `recommend to do something` → ✓ `recommend doing something`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-recommend)
*   **consider：**✗ `consider to do something` → ✓ `consider doing something`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-consider-doing-something)
*   **avoid**：後面接動詞時，那個動詞一定是 -ing。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-avoid-doing-something)
*   **mind**：請人幫忙時，✗ `Would you mind to send me…?` → ✓ `Would you mind sending me…?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-mind)

> 接 to 的多半是「還沒做、打算去做」：decide、plan、hope、expect、promise。這只是幫忙記的傾向，例外很多，最後還是要背。

否定放在 to 或 -ing 前面：`We decided not to renew the contract.`、`Please consider not printing this e-mail.`。

#### 兩種都行的動詞：注意 would like、would prefer

prefer、like、love、hate 接 to 或 -ing 都可以，兩種意思只差一點點。但前面加了 would 或 'd，就只能接 to，不接 -ing。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/hate-like-love-and-prefer) prefer 後面也不能直接接原形：✗ `I prefer drive`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prefer)

*   `The supplier agreed to lower its prices.`（供應商同意降價。）
*   `We can't afford to lose this client.`（我們失去不起這個客戶。）
*   `The director suggested postponing the launch.`（主管建議延後上市。）
*   `Would you mind sending me the updated file?`（可以麻煩你把更新的檔案寄給我嗎？）
*   `The candidate denied copying the report.`（應徵者否認抄襲報告。）
*   `Sales continued to grow in the third quarter.`＝`Sales continued growing…`（第三季業績持續成長。）
*   `I would prefer to meet in the afternoon.`（我比較想約下午見面。）有 would，只能接 to。
*   `We decided not to renew the contract.`（我們決定不續約。）

*【同一個動詞，兩種意思】*

## 接 to 和接 -ing 意思不同：remember、forget、stop、try、regret

這五個動詞接 to 或 -ing 都合文法，但意思不一樣。抓一個大方向：**接 -ing 多半指已經發生、正在進行的事；接 to 多半指還沒做、接下來要做的事**。這是幫忙記的傾向，每個字還是要看下表。

| 動詞 | ＋ -ing | ＋ to＋原形 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| remember | 記得「做過」某件事（回想過去）<br>`I remember meeting her at the trade fair.` | 記得「要去做」（沒忘記）<br>`Remember to save your work.` |
| forget | 忘記「做過」某件事（例如 never forget）<br>`I'll never forget winning my first contract.` | 忘了「要去做」<br>`He forgot to lock the door.` |
| stop | 停止原本在做的事<br>`We've stopped using plastic bags.` | 停下來，**為了去做**另一件事<br>`We stopped to have something to eat.` |
| try | 試試看某個方法（特別是拿來解決問題的方法）有沒有用<br>`Have you tried turning it off and on again?` | 努力想做到（很難，不一定成功）<br>`I'm trying to learn Japanese.` |
| regret | 後悔做過（或沒做）某件事<br>`I regret not checking the figures.` | 正式場合「很遺憾要告知」<br>`We regret to inform you…` |

*【叫別人去做】*

## 動詞＋受詞＋to、使役動詞＋受詞＋原形

這一段的動詞後面先接一個「人」（受詞），再接那個人要做的動作。差別在動作前面**要不要加 to**。

| 句型 | 動詞 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 動詞＋受詞＋to＋原形 | `ask`、`advise`、`allow`、`encourage`、`require`、`remind`、`tell`、`expect`、`invite`、`persuade`、`want`、`would like`、`get` | `The manager asked us to check the figures again.` |
| 動詞＋受詞＋原形（不加 to） | `make`（逼、使）、`let`（讓、准許）、`have`（叫人去做） | `The manager made us check the figures again.` |
| to 可加可不加 | `help` | `This tool helps us (to) save time.` |

*   接「受詞＋to＋原形」的還有 `order`、`warn`、`enable`、`force`、`teach`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verbs-followed-infinitive)
*   `require` 也是這個句型：`Bringing up children often requires you to put their needs first.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/require)

#### make、let、have：受詞後面接原形

**使役動詞**是「使、叫、讓別人去做某事」的動詞，後面接受詞，再接原形（不加 to）：

*   **make、let**：主動句裡後面一定先有受詞，再接原形。✗ `They made us to wait.` → ✓ `They made us wait.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-infinitive-or-verb-ing)
*   **have**：have＋受詞＋原形，是叫某人去做某事：`I’ll have Harry book you a taxi.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/have-something-done)

兩個要小心的地方：

*   **make 改成被動，to 要加回來**。被動一定用帶 to 的不定詞：`The people were made to wait outside…`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/make)
*   **let（允許）這個意思不用被動，改用 be allowed to。**✗ `We weren’t let (to) take photographs` → ✓ `We weren’t allowed to take photographs`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/let-let-s)
*   **allow、permit 要接 to**。allow 和 let 意思接近，但 allow、permit 後面是直接受詞＋to＋原形。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/allow-permit-or-let)

#### help 和 get

*   **help** 後面接原形或 to＋原形都可以，但不接 -ing：✗ `I helped Mary doing her homework.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-help)
*   **get** 意思跟 have 接近（叫人、說服人去做），但接人的時候要加 to：`They got me to talk to the police`。get＋受詞＋-ing 也有促使某事發生的意思，接東西時是「讓它動起來」：`Can you help me get this printer working?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/get)

> make／let／have＋人＋原形；get／ask／allow＋人＋to＋原形；help＋人＋（to）原形。

have 後面接「東西＋過去分詞」（`have the printer repaired`）是請別人把東西處理好，那是被動的用法，放在〈被動語態〉這課。

另外，advise、allow 這兩個字沒有受詞時，後面可以直接接 -ing：`I'd advise waiting until tomorrow.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/advise)、`Smoking is not allowed in this restaurant.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/allow)。有「人」當受詞時才是 advise／allow＋人＋to。

*   `Our policy requires all visitors to wear a badge.`（公司規定所有訪客都要配戴識別證。）
*   `The trainer encouraged new staff to ask questions.`（講師鼓勵新人發問。）
*   `Please remind me to call the supplier tomorrow.`（請提醒我明天打給供應商。）
*   `The supervisor made everyone redo the safety test.`（主管叫大家重考安全測驗。）
*   `Everyone was made to redo the safety test.`（大家被要求重考安全測驗。）被動，加 to。
*   `Our manager let us leave early on Friday.`（經理讓我們週五提早下班。）
*   `I'll have my assistant send you the agenda.`（我會請助理把議程寄給您。）
*   `We finally got the vendor to confirm the delivery date.`（我們終於讓廠商確認了交期。）
*   `This checklist helps new employees (to) learn the system.`（這份清單幫新人熟悉系統。）

*【這個 to 是介系詞】*

## 介系詞後面接 -ing：look forward to、be committed to、be used to

第一段說過，介系詞（in、on、at、for、of、about、before、after、instead of……）後面的動詞用 -ing。最容易錯的是**介系詞剛好是 to** 的時候：看起來像不定詞的 to，其實後面要接名詞或 -ing。

look forward to 裡的 to 是介系詞，所以後面一定接名詞片語或 -ing：✗ `looking forward to go` → ✓ `looking forward to going`。正式信件的結尾 `I look forward to hearing from you soon.` 就是這個句型。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/look-forward-to)

> to 是介系詞的片語要整組背：look forward to、be committed to、be dedicated to、be／get used to，後面接 -ing。

拿不準時可以在 to 後面換成名詞試試（look forward to the holidays 放得進名詞）。但這只是輔助檢查，不是規則。兩個例外：

*   `agree` 同時有 agree to sth 和 agree to do sth，放得進名詞，接動詞卻還是原形：`he agreed to pay $60,000 in damages`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/agree)
*   `due to`＋名詞是「因為」：`The bus was delayed due to heavy snow.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/due-to) 但 `be due to do` 是「預定要做」，接原形。

所以碰到下面這幾組，還是以背下來的片語為準：

*   `be committed to`（致力於）：可以說 `We are committed to quality.`，接動詞時用 -ing：`We are committed to withdrawing our troops by the end of the year.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/committed)
*   `be dedicated to`（專心投入於）：`The Green Party is dedicated to protecting the environment.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dedicated)
*   `be eager to`（很想去做）：這個 to 是不定詞，接原形：`She sounded very eager to meet you.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/eager)

#### used to do 對 be used to doing

長得很像、意思完全不同的一組：

##### used to＋原形：以前常常、現在不再

*   指過去的事，現在已經不是這樣
*   形式不變，前面不加 be：✗ `We are used to go`、✗ `We were used to go`
*   `Mr. Huang used to work in Tokyo.`（黃先生以前在東京上班，現在不是了。）

##### be used to＋名詞／-ing：習慣了

*   意思是「習慣」或「熟悉」，可以指過去、現在或未來
*   後面接名詞片語、代名詞或動詞的 -ing
*   get used to 是「漸漸習慣」
*   `I'm used to working late.`（我習慣加班到很晚。）

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/used-to)、[英國文化協會（be／get used to）](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2-grammar/different-uses-of-used-to)

used to 的否定與問句寫 use，不寫 used：`didn't use to`、`Did you use to…?`。很多人認為帶 -d 的寫法是錯的，考試不要用。

*   `We look forward to working with you.`（期待與您合作。）
*   `We look forward to receiving your payment.`（期待收到您的付款。）
*   `Our company is committed to reducing waste.`（本公司致力於減少浪費。）
*   `After two years in sales, she is used to dealing with difficult clients.`（做了兩年業務，她已經習慣應付難搞的客戶。）
*   `The new staff quickly got used to using the system.`（新人很快就習慣了這套系統。）
*   `This building used to be a factory.`（這棟大樓以前是工廠。）
*   `The team is interested in testing the new software.`（團隊有興趣試用新軟體。）
*   `After signing the contract, the client sent a deposit.`（簽約後，客戶付了訂金。）

## 不定詞表目的；形容詞＋to；too…to 與 enough to

#### 不定詞表目的：to、in order to、so as to

表達目的（回答「為什麼」）用 to＋原形，也可以換成下面兩種：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/infinitives)

*   `in order to`，否定是 `in order not to`。寫作比口語常用。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/in-order-to)
*   `so as to`，否定是 `so as not to`。

中文說「為了……」，很容易寫成 for＋-ing。表達目的或意圖不用 for＋-ing，要用 to＋原形：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/for-ing)

*   ✗ `for visiting my aunt`、✗ `for visit my aunt` → ✓ `to visit my aunt`
*   for＋-ing 是講東西的功能：`I need something for storing CDs.`

後面要接整個子句（主詞＋動詞）時改用 so that，放在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉這課。

#### 形容詞＋to＋原形

後面接 to＋原形的形容詞分兩組：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/infinitives)

*   able、unable、eager、ready、willing、likely、due、prepared、keen 這類：`I'm ready to go to bed.`
*   pleased、happy、sorry、delighted、surprised 這類，to 不定詞說明產生這種感覺的原因：`John was surprised to see me.`

`It is difficult／easy／possible…` 這種句子要講「對誰而言」，用 for：`It was difficult for us to hear what she was saying.`

#### too…to 與 enough to

*   **too＋形容詞＋to**：太……以致於不能，例如 `The water was too cold to swim in.`；too 的意思是「超過需要的」，跟只是加強語氣的 very 不同：`She is too careful.` 是「她小心過頭了」。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/too)
*   **形容詞＋enough＋to**：夠……可以。enough 放在形容詞、副詞**後面**：✗ `Is this box enough big?` → ✓ `Is this box big enough?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/enough)
*   **enough＋名詞＋to**：放在名詞**前面**，例如 `There isn't enough bread to make sandwiches.`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/using-enough)

*   `We left early to avoid the traffic.`（我們提早出發，好避開車潮。）
*   `The meeting was moved to Room 5 in order to fit everyone in.`（會議改到五號會議室，好讓大家都坐得下。）
*   `Please speak quietly so as not to disturb the other teams.`（請小聲說話，以免打擾其他團隊。）
*   `The new hires are eager to start their first project.`（新人很想開始第一個專案。）
*   `We are pleased to announce the opening of our new branch.`（我們很高興宣布新分店開幕。）
*   `The room was too small to hold forty people.`（會議室太小，坐不下四十人。）
*   `The budget isn't large enough to cover the trip.`（預算不夠支付這趟出差。）
*   `We don't have enough staff to open on Sundays.`（我們人手不夠，週日不能營業。）

### 重點整理

*   不定詞是 to＋原形、動名詞是 -ing 當名詞，兩者都不能當句子的主要動詞。-ing 可以當主詞（動詞用單數）、動詞的受詞、介系詞的受詞；不定詞和 -ing 都可以當補語（Our priority is to hire…、One of my tasks is answering e-mails.）；不定詞當主詞時常用虛主詞 It is…to 的句型。
*   動詞後面接 to 還是 -ing 由前面的動詞決定。只接 to：decide、plan、agree、expect、hope、offer、refuse、manage、afford、promise。只接 -ing：avoid、consider、finish、suggest、recommend、postpone、delay、mind、deny、enjoy。兩種都行：begin、start、continue、prefer（would prefer 只接 to）。
*   意思會變的：remember／forget to（要去做）對 -ing（做過）；stop to（停下來去做）對 -ing（不再做）；try to（努力做）對 -ing（試試看）；regret to（遺憾告知）對 -ing（後悔做過）。
*   ask、advise、allow、encourage、require、remind、get＋人＋to＋原形；make、let、have＋人＋原形；help＋人＋（to）原形。make 被動要加 to（be made to），let（允許）不用被動（改用 be allowed to）。suggest 不能接「人＋to」。
*   介系詞後面接 -ing。to 是介系詞的片語：look forward to、be committed to、be dedicated to、be／get used to＋-ing；used to＋原形是「以前常常」。這幾組要整組背；「to 後面放得進名詞」只能當輔助檢查（agree to、be due to 是例外）。
*   表目的用 to、in order to、so as to（否定 not 放 to 前面），不用 for＋-ing。形容詞＋to（eager、ready、due、pleased to）；It is difficult for 人 to…；too＋形容詞＋to；形容詞＋enough＋to；enough＋名詞。
