*【動詞變成的形容詞】*

## 分詞當形容詞：-ing 是主動，過去分詞是被動

**分詞**是動詞的兩種變形：**現在分詞**就是 -ing 形（`rising`、`remaining`），**過去分詞**是規則動詞加 -ed（`updated`、`signed`）、不規則動詞各有各的樣子（`broken`、`written`、`chosen`）。它們跟〈不定詞與動名詞〉那課的 to＋原形、-ing 一樣，都不能自己當句子的主要動詞。這類形式叫**非限定形式**，不顯示時態、人稱或單複數，典型的有三種：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/finite-and-non-finite-verbs)

*   不定詞（帶 to 或不帶 to）：`to go`、`go`
*   -ing 形：`going`
*   -ed 形（過去分詞）：`gone`

分詞最常見的用法是當形容詞，規則動詞、不規則動詞的 -ing 形與 -ed 形都可以：`boiling water`（滾水）、`smoked salmon`（燻鮭魚）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjectives-forms) 兩種怎麼選：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/participle-clauses)

*   **現在分詞（-ing）**：意思接近主動的動詞。
*   **過去分詞**：通常是被動的意思。

> 問一句：這個名詞是「自己在做」這個動作，還是「被人做了」？自己做用 -ing，被做用過去分詞。

| 名詞片語 | 名詞和動作的關係 | 意思 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rising costs` | 成本自己在上升（主動、正在進行） | 不斷上漲的成本 |
| `a growing number of customers` | 數量自己在增加 | 越來越多的顧客 |
| `the remaining seats` | 座位還留著（主動） | 剩下的座位 |
| `the updated schedule` | 時程被人更新了（被動） | 更新過的時程 |
| `a signed contract` | 合約被簽了 | 簽好的合約 |
| `a delayed flight` | 班機被延誤了 | 誤點的班機 |
| `a broken printer` | 印表機被弄壞了（不規則：break → broken） | 壞掉的印表機 |

同一類的 -ing 形容詞還有：`The main problem today is rising prices.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/ing-forms) 不規則動詞的過去分詞要一個一個記，〈被動語態〉這課也會大量用到。

*   `Rising fuel costs have affected our shipping rates.`（不斷上漲的燃料成本影響了我們的運費。）
*   `A growing number of employees work from home on Fridays.`（越來越多員工週五在家上班。）
*   `Please send the remaining payment by March 31.`（請在三月三十一日前付清尾款。）
*   `The revised budget was approved yesterday.`（修正後的預算昨天通過了。）
*   `Passengers on delayed flights will receive meal vouchers.`（誤點班機的乘客會拿到餐券。）
*   `The suggested retail price is $299.`（建議售價是 299 美元。）

*【職場常見，感覺怎麼說】*

## 「讓人覺得」用 -ing，「人覺得」用 -ed：interesting 對 interested

表示情緒的動詞（interest、confuse、satisfy、disappoint、excite、bore、surprise……）變成形容詞時，-ing 和 -ed 意思不同：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/adjectives-ending-ed-ing)[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjectives-forms)

*   **-ed 結尾**：通常描述情緒，說人有什麼感覺。
*   **-ing 結尾**：通常描述引起這種情緒的東西，也就是它帶來的效果。例如一堂 `boring` 的課，會讓你覺得 `bored`。

> -ing：讓人覺得……的（說事情、東西）；-ed：（人）覺得……的。

| 動詞 | -ing：讓人覺得 | -ed：人覺得 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interest` | `an interesting proposal` 有趣的提案 | `I'm interested in the proposal.` 我對提案有興趣 |
| `confuse` | `The instructions are confusing.` 說明讓人看不懂 | `Customers were confused by the new policy.` 顧客被新規定搞糊塗了 |
| `satisfy` | `a satisfying job` 讓人有成就感的工作 | `The client is satisfied with the design.` 客戶對設計很滿意 |
| `disappoint` | `disappointing sales` 讓人失望的業績 | `The manager was disappointed.` 經理很失望 |
| `excite` | `exciting news` 令人興奮的消息 | `The team is excited about the launch.` 團隊對上市很興奮 |
| `bore` | `a boring meeting` 無聊的會議 | `I was bored at the meeting.` 我開會開得很無聊 |
| `surprise` | `surprising results` 出乎意料的結果 | `We were surprised by the results.` 我們對結果很意外 |
| `tire` | `a tiring trip` 累人的出差 | `She was tired after the trip.` 她出差回來很累 |

同一類的成對字還有 `annoyed`／`annoying`。

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/adjectives-ending-ed-ing)

-ed 形容詞後面接的介系詞有固定搭配：

*   `interested in`：`I'm interested in theatre.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-interesting-or-interested)
*   `excited about`：`She was very excited about the visit.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-excited-or-exciting)
*   `satisfied with`：`Are you satisfied with the new arrangement?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/satisfied)
*   `confused by`：`She was momentarily confused by the foreign road signs.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/confused)

#### 人也可以用 -ing，但意思不一樣

-ing 不是只能說東西。一個人或東西是 interesting，就是他讓你覺得 interested。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-interesting-or-interested) 所以 `He was boring.` 文法沒錯，意思是「他這個人很無聊（讓別人覺得無聊）」，不是「他覺得無聊」。

*   ✗ `He didn't enjoy the lesson because he was boring.` → ✓ `…because he was bored.`：他不喜歡那堂課，是因為他**覺得**無聊，錯在意思。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-bored-or-boring)

解題時看兩個線索：

*   **主詞是誰**：主詞是事情、東西（the report、the results、the trip），幾乎都用 -ing。
*   **後面接什麼**：後面有 `with`、`in`、`about`、`by` 帶出「對什麼有感覺」，前面通常就是人在感覺，用 -ed：`satisfied with`、`interested in`、`excited about`、`confused by`。

*   `The keynote speech was very interesting.`（主題演講很有趣。）
*   `Several investors are interested in the project.`（有幾位投資人對這個案子有興趣。）
*   `The quarterly results were disappointing.`（季報結果令人失望。）
*   `Many staff members were confused by the new expense form.`（很多同仁被新的報帳表格搞糊塗了。）
*   `It's an immensely satisfying job.`（這是一份非常有成就感的工作。）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/satisfying)
*   `The instructions are really confusing.`（說明書真的很難懂。）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/confusing)

*【放前面、放後面】*

## 分詞放名詞前後，以及它和關係子句的關係

分詞可以放在名詞前面，也可以放在名詞後面，例如 `Who is that man standing over there?` 的 standing 就放在 man 後面。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/ing-forms) 常見的情況是：

*   **只有一個分詞**：放名詞前面，`the attached file`。
*   **分詞後面還帶著受詞或介系詞片語**：整串放名詞後面，`the file attached to this e-mail`、`the people attending the meeting`。

放在名詞後面的分詞片語，可以看成**關係子句的縮短版**（關係子句是 who、which、that 開頭、修飾前面名詞的子句）。被修飾的名詞在句子裡當主詞、當受詞都可以。下面兩個例子一個主動、一個被動，而且第二個的 the form 是 fill in 的受詞：

*   `The man sitting on the sofa over there is Simon’s brother.`＝The man **who is sitting**…（主動 → -ing）
*   `Don’t forget to fill in the form attached to the letter.`＝…**which is attached** to the letter.（被動 → 過去分詞）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

> 主動：把關係子句的動詞改成 -ing（who is sitting → sitting、anyone who wishes → anyone wishing）。被動：拿掉 who／which＋be，留下過去分詞（which is attached → attached）。

名詞後面的 -ing 片語說的是正在進行、經常性的動作或狀態。過去已經做完的一次性動作，例如 `the engineer who designed the system`（設計了這套系統的工程師），保留關係子句，不縮成 designing：the engineer designing the system 會變成「正在設計這套系統的工程師」。

關係子句本身怎麼寫，放在〈關係詞〉這課。

有些過去分詞習慣放在名詞後面，不放前面：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjective-phrases-position)

*   ✗ `the mentioned issues` → ✓ `Most of the issues mentioned in the documentary are not very important.`
*   ✗ `the shown illustrations` → ✓ `the illustrations shown`

*   `All employees attending the workshop should bring a laptop.`（參加工作坊的員工請帶筆電。）＝who are attending
*   `Orders placed after 3 p.m. will be shipped the next day.`（下午三點後下的訂單隔天出貨。）＝which are placed
*   `The woman leading the project used to work in Singapore.`（帶這個專案的那位女士以前在新加坡工作。）
*   `The report prepared by the finance team shows a small loss.`（財務團隊準備的報告顯示小幅虧損。）
*   `Anyone wishing to join the tour should sign up by Friday.`（想參加導覽的人請在週五前報名。）
*   `The problems mentioned in your e-mail have been fixed.`（您信中提到的問題已經修好了。）

*【把副詞子句縮短】*

## 分詞構句：-ing、過去分詞、having＋過去分詞

**分詞構句**（participle clause）是把「when、because、after 這類連接詞開頭的子句」縮成一個分詞片語，用更精簡的方式說同樣的資訊。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/participle-clauses)

*   **條件**：分詞和主要子句的動詞主詞相同，才能用。例：`Waiting for Ellie, I made some tea.`＝`While I was waiting for Ellie, I made some tea.`
*   **時態**：分詞構句沒有自己的時態，時態看主要子句。
*   **用在哪裡**：主要用在書面文字。

縮短的步驟：

*   **第一步，確認兩個子句主詞相同**。主詞不同還硬縮，就會出現下一段講的錯誤。
*   **第二步，拿掉主詞**（連接詞可以拿掉；when、while、before、after 也可以留著）。
*   **第三步，動詞改成分詞**：主動用 -ing；被動用過去分詞（be 動詞一起拿掉）；比主要子句**先完成**的動作用 having＋過去分詞。否定把 not 放最前面。

| 種類 | 原本的句子 | 分詞構句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 → -ing | `When I reviewed the report, I found two errors.` | `Reviewing the report, I found two errors.`／`When reviewing the report, I found…` |
| 被動 → 過去分詞 | `The company was founded in 1998. It now has 500 employees.` | `Founded in 1998, the company now has 500 employees.` |
| 先完成 → having＋過去分詞 | `After she had finished the project, she took a week off.` | `Having finished the project, she took a week off.` |
| 否定 → not 放前面 | `Because he didn't know the address, he called the office.` | `Not knowing the address, he called the office.` |
| 結果 → , -ing | `The storm closed the airport, and this caused long delays.` | `The storm closed the airport, causing long delays.` |

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/participle-clauses)、[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

表格之外再補幾點：

*   **現在分詞構句能表示的關係**：動作的結果（`The bomb exploded, destroying the building.`）、原因、同時發生的動作。
*   **過去分詞構句**：`Worried by the news, she called the hospital.`、`Helped by local volunteers, staff at the museum have spent many years sorting and cataloguing more than 100,000 photographs.`
*   **完成式分詞構句**（having＋過去分詞）：表示它描述的動作，在主要子句的動作之前已經完成。`After having spent six hours at the hospital, they eventually came home.`
*   **前面可以加的字**：分詞構句也常接在 before、after、instead of、on、since、when、while 這類連接詞和介系詞後面。
*   **否定**：`Not wanting to hurt his feelings, I avoided the question.`

*   `When reviewing the contract, please check the payment terms.`（審合約時請檢查付款條件。）
*   `Having finished the audit, the team sent its report to the board.`（查核做完後，團隊把報告送交董事會。）
*   `Founded in 1985, the firm now has offices in twelve countries.`（這家公司 1985 年創立，現在在十二個國家設有辦公室。）
*   `Encouraged by the strong sales, the company opened two more stores.`（受到業績亮眼的鼓舞，公司又開了兩家店。）
*   `Not having received the invoice, we could not process the payment.`（因為沒收到發票，我們無法付款。）
*   `The new system processes orders automatically, saving us several hours a day.`（新系統自動處理訂單，每天替我們省下好幾個小時。）

*【主詞要對得上】*

## 懸垂分詞：分詞構句最常見的錯

上一段說，分詞構句用在分詞和主要子句的動詞**主詞相同**的時候；非限定子句一般也常用在主詞跟主要子句的主詞相同時。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite) 所以讀的人會把**逗號後面的主詞**當成分詞的主詞。主詞對不上，句子就會變成別的意思，這種錯誤叫**懸垂分詞**（dangling participle，分詞「懸」在那裡，找不到自己的主詞）：

##### 主詞對不上

*   `Walking into the office, the phone started ringing.`  
    變成「電話走進辦公室」。
*   `Having reviewed the proposal, the budget was approved.`  
    變成「預算審完了提案」。
*   `Located near the station, many travelers stay at the hotel.`  
    變成「旅客位在車站附近」。

##### 改成主詞相同，或還原成子句

*   `Walking into the office, I heard the phone ringing.`  
    或 `As I walked into the office, the phone started ringing.`
*   `Having reviewed the proposal, the board approved the budget.`
*   `Located near the station, the hotel is popular with travelers.`

不要誤判的一種：`Given the delay, …`、`Including tax, …`、`Following the meeting, …`、`Regarding your order, …` 開頭的句子不算懸垂分詞，因為 given、including、following、regarding 在辭典裡都另外列為**介系詞**（見下一段），用法跟 after、about 一樣，不需要跟主要子句的主詞對上。

**🧩 小練習：** 下面哪一句文法正確？

</details>

*【商業書信的老面孔】*

## 多益常見的分詞片語：attached、enclosed、including、included、following、regarding、given

這幾個字在多益的電子郵件、公告、廣告裡很常見。有的仍是分詞，有的在辭典裡已經另外列成**介系詞**，用法要分開記：

| 字 | 用法 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| attached | （附在電子郵件裡）附上的 | `Please see the attached file.`／`the file attached to this e-mail`／`Please find attached our latest price list.` |
| enclosed | （附在信封裡）隨信附上的 | `The enclosed card is for Julia.`／`Please find enclosed a cheque…` |
| including | 介系詞：包括……；放在被包括的東西**前面** | `The price is $300, including tax.` |
| included | 形容詞：（某樣東西）包括在內；放在名詞**後面** | `The price is $300, tax included.` |
| following | 介系詞：在……之後；the following 是「接下來的、下列的」 | `Following the meeting, there will be lunch.`／`the following morning` |
| regarding | 介系詞：關於（正式）；後面不加 to | `I am writing regarding your order.` |
| given | 介系詞：考慮到…… | `Given the tight schedule, we should start now.` |

每個字再細一點：

*   **enclosed**：跟信一起裝在信封裡寄給某人。`The enclosed card is for Julia.`；正式的寫法 `Please find enclosed a cheque in settlement of your invoice.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/enclosed)
*   **attach**：有「隨信寄上」的用法，`I attach (= am sending, usually with a letter) a copy of our latest report.`；the attached file、Please find attached 是同理的寫法。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/attach)
*   **including**：介系詞。`Eight people, including two children, were injured in the explosion.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/including)
*   **included**：形容詞，只放在名詞後面。`The trip cost a total of £250, insurance included.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/included)
*   **following**：不加 the 時意思是「在……之後、回應……、因為……」；the following 加名詞是「下一個」，不加名詞時用來帶出一串清單。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/following-or-the-following)
*   **regarding**：介系詞，意思是 about，屬於正式用法；後面從來不接 to。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/regarding)[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-regarding)
*   **given**：當介系詞，意思是知道或考慮到某件事。`Given his age, he's a remarkably fast runner.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/given)

*   `Please find attached the agenda for Monday's meeting.`（附件是週一會議的議程。）
*   `Please review the enclosed brochure for details.`（詳情請看隨信附上的手冊。）
*   `All staff, including managers, must complete the training.`（所有員工，包括主管，都要完成訓練。）
*   `The room rate is $180, breakfast included.`（房價 180 美元，含早餐。）
*   `Following the merger, the company changed its name.`（合併之後，公司改了名字。）
*   `Please bring the following: your ID, a pen, and your résumé.`（請帶下列物品：身分證、筆和履歷。）
*   `Regarding your question about delivery, we ship within two days.`（關於您詢問的出貨，我們兩天內寄出。）
*   `Given the bad weather, the outdoor event will be held indoors.`（考慮到天氣不好，戶外活動改在室內舉行。）

## 綜合練習：照多益第五部分的樣子

**🧩 小練習：** The slow Internet connection in the branch office is very ______.

- (B) Regarded
- (C) Regard

### 重點整理

*   分詞當形容詞：名詞自己做這個動作用 -ing（rising costs、the remaining seats），名詞被做用過去分詞（the updated schedule、a signed contract）。
*   情緒形容詞：-ing 是「讓人覺得」，說事情、東西（an interesting proposal、disappointing results）；-ed 是「人覺得」（interested in、satisfied with、excited about、confused by）。主詞是東西幾乎都用 -ing；人用 -ing 是「這個人讓別人覺得……」。
*   分詞片語放名詞後面，可以看成關係子句的縮短版（名詞當主詞、受詞都行）：主動把動詞改成 -ing（the people attending the meeting、anyone wishing to join），被動拿掉 who／which＋be 留下過去分詞（the form attached to the letter）；過去做完的一次性動作不縮成 -ing。mentioned、shown 這類習慣放名詞後面。
*   分詞構句：兩個子句主詞相同時可以縮。主動用 -ing、被動用過去分詞（Founded in 1998, …）、先完成用 having＋過去分詞、否定 not 放前面；when、while、before、after 可以留著（When reviewing…）。
*   懸垂分詞：分詞的主詞要等於逗號後面的主詞，對不上就要改寫（Walking into the office, I heard…）。given、including、following、regarding 是介系詞，不受這條限制。
*   信件常用：Please find attached／enclosed…、the attached file；including tax（放前面）對 tax included（放後面）；following＝after、the following＝下列的；regarding 不加 to；Given the…＝考慮到。
