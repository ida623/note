## 形容詞的位置：好幾個怎麼排、哪些只能放後面

形容詞的基本位置（名詞前面、`be`、`seem`、`look` 這類連綴動詞後面；連綴動詞後面填形容詞，不填副詞）見〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉；`interesting` 和 `interested` 這類 -ing／-ed 形容詞見〈分詞與分詞構句〉。

#### 好幾個形容詞排在一起：先放意見，再放事實

名詞前面有不只一個形容詞時，通常照一定的順序排：

*   描述意見或態度的（例如 `amazing`）通常放最前面。
*   比較中性、講事實的（例如 `red`）放後面。

不特別強調哪一個時，最常見的順序如下：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjectives-order)

| 順序 | 種類 | 例字 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 意見（opinion） | `unusual`、`lovely`、`beautiful` |
| 2 | 大小（size） | `big`、`small`、`tall` |
| 3 | 質地等物理性質（physical quality） | `thin`、`rough`、`untidy` |
| 4 | 形狀（shape） | `round`、`square`、`rectangular` |
| 5 | 新舊、年紀（age） | `young`、`old`、`youthful` |
| 6 | 顏色（colour） | `blue`、`red`、`pink` |
| 7 | 產地（origin） | `Dutch`、`Japanese`、`Turkish` |
| 8 | 材質（material） | `metal`、`wood`、`plastic` |
| 9 | 類型（type） | `general-purpose`、`four-sided`、`U-shaped` |
| 10 | 用途（purpose） | `cleaning`、`hammering`、`cooking` |

比較短的記法：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/adjective-order)

*   一般意見（`good`、`nice`）放在特定意見（`comfortable`、`delicious`）前面。
*   意見通常放在描述前面。
*   名詞前面放超過三個形容詞非常少見。

不必背十格，記住「意見最前、大小新舊在中間、產地材質靠近名詞」就夠解題。

*   `We ordered a comfortable black leather chair for the reception area.`（我們替接待區訂了一張舒服的黑色皮椅。）意見→顏色→材質。
*   `The meeting will be held in the large rectangular room on the third floor.`（會議在三樓那間長方形的大會議室。）大小→形狀。
*   `She bought a beautiful old wooden desk for her office.`（她替辦公室買了一張漂亮的舊木桌。）意見→新舊→材質。
*   `Our supplier sent us a small plastic sample case.`（供應商寄來一個小的塑膠樣品盒。）大小→材質。
*   `The firm hired an experienced young Japanese designer.`（公司請了一位經驗豐富的年輕日本設計師。）意見→年紀→產地。
*   `We need a reliable new delivery van.`（我們需要一台可靠的新貨車。）意見→新舊；`delivery` 是名詞當修飾，緊貼著 `van`。

#### a- 開頭的形容詞只放在連綴動詞後面

a- 開頭的形容詞不能放在名詞前面，要放在 `be`、`seem`、`become`、`feel`、`smell`、`taste` 這類連綴動詞後面。常見的有：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjective-phrases-position)

*   `awake`、`alive`、`asleep`
*   `aboard`（在飛機、船、車上）
*   `ablaze`（著火）

要放名詞前面，就換一個意思相近的字：

*   `alive` → `live`
*   `alone` → `lone`
*   `ablaze` → `blazing`

只放連綴動詞後面的還有 `afraid`、`alone`、`glad`、`ready`、`sure`、`unable`、`ill`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/adjective-order)

反過來，有些形容詞**只放名詞前面**：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjective-phrases-position)

*   限定後面名詞的字：`main`、`only`、`chief`、`principal`、`sole`
*   講時間先後的：`former`、`present`、`future`
*   ✗ `The reason is main.` → ✓ `The main reason why the cinema closed…`

`mentioned`、`shown` 這類習慣放在名詞後面的過去分詞，在〈分詞與分詞構句〉。

*   `The night guard was still awake when the alarm went off.`（警報響的時候，夜班警衛還醒著。）
*   `All passengers were aboard by 7:40.`（七點四十分前乘客全部上船了。）
*   `Ms. Lin was alone in the office on Friday afternoon.`（週五下午林小姐一個人在辦公室。）
*   `A lone visitor was waiting in the lobby.`（大廳有一位訪客獨自在等。）放名詞前面用 `lone`。
*   `The main reason for the delay was a shortage of parts.`（延誤的主因是零件短缺。）
*   `Our former manager now runs a consulting firm.`（我們的前經理現在開顧問公司。）

## 形容詞＋介系詞：responsible for、familiar with、eligible for…

形容詞後面要接名詞時，常常要先接一個固定的介系詞：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/adjectives-prepositions)

*   哪個形容詞配哪個介系詞，沒有文法規則可循，最好整組一起背。
*   介系詞後面接名詞或動名詞（-ing 形）。

下表是多益職場句子常見的形容詞＋介系詞（`be satisfied with`、`be based on`、`be located in` 這類見〈被動語態〉；`interested in`、`excited about` 見〈分詞與分詞構句〉）：

| 搭配 | 意思 | 例句 | 出處 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `responsible for` | 負責……；是……的原因 | `Ms. Ho is responsible for the training budget.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-responsible) |
| `familiar with` | （人）熟悉…… | `Are all staff familiar with the new software?` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/familiar) |
| `eligible for`／`eligible to`＋原形 | 有資格得到……／有資格做…… | `Full-time employees are eligible for the annual bonus.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/eligible) |
| `aware of` | 知道、察覺到…… | `Were you aware of the change in the delivery date?` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/aware) |
| `capable of`＋名詞／-ing | 有能力…… | `The new machine is capable of printing 60 pages a minute.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/capable) |
| `similar to` | 跟……相似 | `Our proposal is similar to last year's.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/similar) |
| `consistent with` | 跟……一致 | `The figures are consistent with our forecast.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/consistent) |
| `dependent on` | 取決於……；依賴…… | `The bonus is dependent on sales performance.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dependent) |
| `compatible with` | 跟……相容 | `This software is not compatible with older printers.` | [出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/compatible) |
| `good at`、`famous for`、`experienced in`、`proud of`、`worried about` | 擅長、以……出名、在……有經驗、以……為傲、擔心 | `We want someone who's experienced in sales.` | [出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/adjectives-prepositions) |

表中每一列的出處在最右欄。

兩個要注意的地方：

*   `responsible` 後面只接 for：✗ `responsible of` → ✓ `responsible for`。
*   `familiar` 看誰熟悉誰：人 `familiar with` 東西（人熟悉它），東西 `familiar to` 人（東西對人來說很眼熟）：`I'm sorry, I'm not familiar with your poetry.`、`The street was familiar to me.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/familiar)

## 副詞的位置與容易混淆的副詞

副詞的基本位置（主詞和動詞之間、第一個助動詞後面、表示方式的副詞放句尾）見〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉；always、usually 這類頻率副詞的位置見〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉。下面幾組副詞長得像、意思不同，常被拿來當陷阱選項。

#### 加了 -ly 意思就變了的六組

| 沒有 -ly | 有 -ly | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hard` 努力地、用力地 | `hardly` 幾乎不 | `She works hard.` 對 `She hardly works.` |
| `late` 晚、遲 | `lately` 最近 | `The train arrived late.` 對 `Sales have been slow lately.` |
| `near` 在附近、靠近 | `nearly` 幾乎、差不多 | `The hotel is near the airport.` 對 `We have nearly finished.` |
| `high` 高高地（講高度） | `highly` 非常；評價高 | `The drone flew high.` 對 `a highly paid job` |
| `close` 靠近 | `closely` 密切地；仔細地 | `Don't stand too close to the machine.` 對 `We work closely with the police.` |
| `most` 最（最高級）；大多數 | `mostly` 主要是、大部分是 | `Which proposal did you like most?` 對 `The staff are mostly engineers.` |

*   **hard／hardly**：形容詞 hard 的副詞還是 `hard`，不是 hardly；`hardly` 是「勉強」或「幾乎不」。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/hard-or-hardly)
*   **late**：是副詞也是形容詞，意思是 early 的相反。
*   **lately**：也是副詞，意思是「最近」；用在狀態或反覆發生的事，大多配現在完成式。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/late-or-lately)
*   hardly、lately 跟 hard、late 意思不同。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/adverbials-manner)
*   **nearly**：幾乎、沒有完全：`I've nearly finished that book you lent me.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/nearly)
*   **near**：距離不遠：`The hotel is near the airport.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/near)
*   **high**：當副詞是離地面很高。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/high)
*   **highly**：非常、程度很高：`highly paid`、`a highly profitable line of products`；也有「給予高評價」的意思：`Her colleagues think very highly of her work.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/highly)
*   **close**：當副詞是位置或時間不遠，常接 to：`Don't get too close to that dog`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/close)
*   **closely**：關係密切，或仔細、注意細節：`We are working closely with the police.`、`Pollution levels are closely monitored.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/closely)
*   **most／mostly：**`mostly` 是副詞，意思是「主要」；不用 mostly 代替 most 或 the most：✗ `What I liked mostly…` → ✓ `What I liked most were the beautiful beaches.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/most)

#### also、too、either：放哪裡、肯定還是否定

*   `also` 通常放在副詞的中間位置：主詞和主要動詞之間、第一個助動詞後面、be 動詞後面：`She also speaks Japanese.`
*   `too` 通常放句尾，而且通常不放在助動詞後面：`I'll have the chicken too.`
*   兩個**否定**的事接在一起，用 `either`，不用 also、as well、too：`I don't think Dave is either.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/also-as-well-or-too)
*   `either` 當副詞用在否定的動詞後面：`She doesn't like eggs and she doesn't like fish either.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/either)

#### already、yet、still：已經、還沒、還是

這三個字配現在完成式的用法見〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉。另外三條規則：

*   `already` 用在肯定句和問句，通常不用在否定句；也不放在動詞和直接受詞中間：✗ `I’ve made already the coffee.` → ✓ `I’ve already made the coffee.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/already)
*   `yet` 大多用在現在完成式的否定句或問句，通常放句尾；已經發生的事不用 yet，要用 already。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/yet)
*   `still` 強調「還在持續」；在否定句裡放在助動詞前面，表示早該發生卻還沒發生：`she still hasn’t taken any driving lessons`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/still)

*   `The new intern hardly spoke during his first meeting.`（新來的實習生第一次開會幾乎沒說話。）
*   `Have you been busy lately?`（你最近忙嗎？）
*   `Our sales manager is highly respected in the industry.`（我們的業務經理在業界很受敬重。）
*   `The auditors checked the receipts closely.`（稽核人員仔細檢查了收據。）
*   `The visitors were mostly from Japan.`（訪客大部分來自日本。）
*   `The finance team didn't approve the plan, and the legal team didn't either.`（財務部沒核准這個計畫，法務部也沒有。）
*   `The landlord still hasn't fixed the air conditioner.`（房東到現在還沒修冷氣。）

*【-er、more，還有幾個不規則】*

## 比較級與最高級怎麼變

**比較級**（comparative）拿兩個東西比，說哪個「更……」；**最高級**（superlative）在一群裡挑出「最……」的那一個。也就是說，比較級是一個跟另一個比，最高級是一個跟它所屬的整群比。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

用 -er 還是 more，看形容詞有幾個音節（念起來有幾拍）：

| 形容詞 | 比較級 | 最高級 | 例字 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 一個音節 | 加 `-er` | 加 `-est` | `cheap → cheaper → cheapest` |
| 一個音節、`-e` 結尾 | 加 `-r` | 加 `-st` | `safe → safer → safest` |
| 一個音節、一個母音＋一個子音結尾 | 雙寫最後的子音再加 `-er` | 雙寫再加 `-est` | `big → bigger → biggest`、`hot → hotter` |
| 兩個音節、子音＋`-y` 結尾 | `y` 改 `i` 加 `-er` | `y` 改 `i` 加 `-est` | `busy → busier → busiest`、`easy → easier` |
| `-ful` 結尾等很多兩個音節的字 | `more` | `most` | `useful → more useful`、`careful → more careful` |
| 三個音節以上 | `more` | `most` | `expensive → more expensive → most expensive` |

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

兩個音節、表裡沒列到的字：

*   大部分用 `more`；三個音節以上全部用 `more`。
*   有些兩種都可以，例如 `common`、`narrow`、`polite`、`simple`、`pleasant`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/comparative-adjectives)
*   另一些也可以加 -er／-est，例如 `clever`、`quiet`、`narrow`、`simple`。

#### 不規則的比較級

| 原級 | 比較級 | 最高級 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `good`（形容詞）／`well`（副詞） | `better` | `best` |
| `bad`（形容詞）／`badly`（副詞） | `worse` | `worst` |
| `far` | `farther`／`further` | `farthest`／`furthest` |
| `little`（量少） | `less` | `least` |

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

*   副詞 `well` 的比較級、最高級跟形容詞 good 一樣是 `better`、`best`；`badly` 是 `worse`、`worst`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adverbs)
*   `less` 是比較的字，用來講減少的數量或程度。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/less)
*   `least` 是比其他任何東西或任何人都少。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/least)

**further 在多益常當「更多的、進一步的」**：further 也有「更多、額外的」的意思：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/further)

*   `If you have any further problems, let me know.`
*   `This shop will be closed until further notice.`
*   `for further information`（詳情）、`until further notice`（另行通知前）

#### 兩條要記的禁忌

*   **more 和 -er 不能一起用**：more、most 不跟 -er、-est 字尾疊在一起：✗ `a more better life`、✗ `the most biggest`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)
*   **副詞的比較**：短的副詞用 -er／-est（`fast → faster`、`hard → harder`、`early → earlier`、`late → later`）；兩個音節以上的副詞用 more 和 most：`more carefully`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adverbs)

*   `The new laptop is lighter than the old one.`（新筆電比舊的輕。）
*   `March is our busiest month.`（三月是我們最忙的月份。）
*   `This route is more convenient for most employees.`（這條路線對大多數員工比較方便。）
*   `The second quarter was worse than the first.`（第二季比第一季差。）
*   `For further information, please visit our website.`（詳情請上我們的網站。）
*   `Please drive more carefully in the parking lot.`（在停車場請開慢一點、小心一點。）
*   `The finance team finished earlier than we expected.`（財務部比我們預期的早做完。）

*【多益高頻，句型一次整理】*

## 比較的句型：than、the＋最高級、as…as、倍數、much＋比較級、the＋比較級

#### 比較級＋than

比較級後面接 than，不接 that 或 as。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

選項同時有 `than`、`that`、`then`、`as` 時，往前找有沒有 -er 或 more：有就選 than。

#### the＋最高級，範圍用 in 或 of

*   最高級後面接名詞時，通常要加 the：`This is the best meal I’ve had for a long time.`
*   範圍是**單一的地方或團體**時用 `in`，通常不用 of：`the oldest building in the city`、`the youngest musician in the orchestra`
*   範圍是**指一群人或東西的複數字**時可以用 `of`：`Sarah’s the prettiest of them all.`
*   三個以上相比用最高級，不用比較級。
*   只有兩個相比時，傳統用比較級；非正式場合常聽到用最高級。
*   加強最高級用 `by far`、`easily`、`of all`：`by far the most successful rock band`

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

**one of the＋最高級＋複數名詞：**「最……的之一」是從一群裡挑出一個，所以名詞用複數：

*   `It’s one of the most popular songs.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/one)
*   `The beach at Marmaris is one of the biggest in Turkey.`（不說 the most biggest）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

陷阱選項常見的出法：

*   把名詞換成單數：✗ `one of the largest supplier`
*   把最高級換成比較級。

#### as…as 與 not as…as

兩樣東西在某方面相等時用 as…as，中間放形容詞或副詞的**原級**：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-as-as)

*   肯定：`He’s as tall as his father now.`
*   否定：`not as…as` 或 `not so…as`，`not as…as` 比較常見。
*   中間要放名詞時，一定要用 `much`／`little`＋不可數名詞、`many`／`few`＋複數名詞：`There are as many students in Class 2A as there are in 2B.`
*   一個音節的短形容詞通常不用 less，改用 not as…as：`This new laptop is not as fast as my old one.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

#### 倍數：twice as…as

「是……的兩倍」把 `twice` 放在 as…as 前面：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/twice)

*   `There are twice as many houses in this area as there used to be.`
*   `The state is at least twice as big as England.`

同理，三倍以上用 `three times as…as`、`four times as…as`。

#### 加強比較級：much、far、a lot、even，不用 very

加強與減弱比較級：

*   **加強與減弱**：可以用 `much`、`a lot`、`far`、`even` 或 `rather` 加強比較級；要減弱用 `a little`、`a bit`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)
*   **不用 very**：比較級前面不用 very，要用 very much：✗ `very bigger` → ✓ `London is very much bigger than Naples.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/very)
*   **例句**：`Sales in July were a lot higher than sales in June.`、`The number of registrations has been slightly lower than we expected.`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/modifying-comparatives)

> 比較級前面的空格：much、far、a lot、even、slightly 可以；very 不行。

#### the＋比較級, the＋比較級；越來越……

*   「越……就越……」：一樣性質增加、帶動另一樣也跟著增加時，可以重複 the＋比較級：`The colder it is, the hungrier I get.`
*   「越來越……」：用兩個 -er 形容詞以 and 連起來，或在形容詞前面用 more and more，後面不接 than：`The weather is getting hotter and hotter.`、`I’m getting more and more interested in conservation…`

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-bigger-biggest-more-and-most)

*   `Shipping by sea is much cheaper than shipping by air.`（海運比空運便宜很多。）
*   `Ms. Kao is the most experienced accountant in our department.`（高小姐是我們部門最資深的會計。）
*   `This is one of the busiest ports in the region.`（這是這個區域最繁忙的港口之一。）
*   `This year's profit is twice as high as last year's.`（今年的獲利是去年的兩倍。）
*   `The new model is not as heavy as the old one.`（新機型沒有舊的那麼重。）
*   `We received as many applications as last year.`（我們收到的應徵件跟去年一樣多。）
*   `The earlier you book, the cheaper the tickets are.`（越早訂，票越便宜。）
*   `Our workload is getting heavier and heavier.`（我們的工作量越來越重。）

## so 與 such、too 與 enough

such 和 so 常常都是「很、這麼」的意思，差別在詞性：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/so-or-such)

*   `such` 是限定詞（放在名詞前面、限定它指哪個或多少的字，例如 a、the、this），後面接名詞片語。
*   `so` 是副詞，後面接形容詞或副詞。

| 寫法 | 後面接 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `so` | 形容詞、副詞 | `The presentation was so long.`、`She speaks so quickly.` |
| `such`（`a`／`an`） | 名詞，或形容詞＋名詞 | `It was such a long presentation.`、`They are such helpful colleagues.` |
| `so` | `much`、`many`、`little`、`few`＋名詞 | `There were so many questions.` |
| `so`／`such`…`that` | 後面接結果 | `It was so cold that the water froze.` |

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/so-or-such)

還要注意：

*   名詞前面就算有形容詞，也用 such，不用 so：✗ `She is so great cook`；有 a／an 時：✗ `a so wonderful kitchen` → ✓ `such a wonderful kitchen`
*   such 後面要有名詞：✗ `That was such unpleasant`
*   `much`、`many`、`little`、`few` 前面用 so，不用 such。
*   so 和 such 這兩種句型常接 that 加子句，說結果是什麼：`He was such a good teacher that we all passed the exam.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/intensifiers-so-such)

**too 與 enough**：too…to 與 enough to 的句型見〈不定詞與動名詞〉。

*   **too**：緊接在形容詞和副詞前面，意思是「超過需要的、太……」；講數量用 `too much`＋不可數名詞、`too many`＋可數名詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/too)
*   **enough＋形容詞**：`enough` 的位置剛好相反：放在形容詞**後面**（修飾形容詞的字多半放前面，enough 是例外）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjective-phrases-position)
*   **enough＋名詞**：放在名詞**前面**，而且名詞前面有 the、my 這類字時要用 `enough of`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/enough)

> so＋形容詞；such a＋形容詞＋名詞；形容詞＋enough；enough＋名詞；too＋形容詞。

*   `The new system is so easy to use.`（新系統好用極了。）
*   `It was such a productive meeting.`（這場會開得很有成效。）
*   `There were so few seats left that we had to stand.`（剩的座位少到我們只能站著。）
*   `The deadline is too close; we need more time.`（期限太近了，我們需要更多時間。）
*   `Is the room large enough for 40 people?`（這間會議室夠容納四十人嗎？）
*   `We don't have enough staff on weekends.`（我們週末人手不夠。）

## 綜合練習：照多益第五部分的樣子

先看選項判斷考什麼：形容詞還是副詞、哪一組副詞、比較級還是最高級、哪個句型，再看空格前後的線索（than、the、as、of、that）。

**🧩 小練習：** Our customer service team responds to complaints ______ than any of our competitors.

- (A) quickly
- (B) most quickly
- (C) more quickly
- (D) more quick

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(C)** 整句：我們客服團隊回應投訴的速度比任何一家競爭對手都快。  
修飾動詞 responds 用副詞，後面有 than，要比較級：more quickly。quickly 是原級；most quickly 是最高級，不配 than；more quick 用的是形容詞。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** Ms. Chen is one of the most ______ consultants in the industry.

- (A) highly
- (B) respected
- (C) respect
- (D) respectfully

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(B)** 整句：陳小姐是業界最受敬重的顧問之一。  
one of the most＋形容詞＋複數名詞：most respected consultants。highly 是副詞，不能直接修飾名詞 consultants。

</details>

### 重點整理

*   好幾個形容詞：意見在前、事實在後；大致是 意見→大小→形狀→新舊→顏色→產地→材質→名詞（a comfortable black leather chair）。asleep、alive、alone、aboard 這類 a- 開頭的形容詞只放連綴動詞後面；main、only、former 只放名詞前面。
*   形容詞＋介系詞要整組記：responsible for、familiar with、eligible for（eligible to＋原形）、aware of、capable of＋-ing、similar to、consistent with、dependent on、compatible with。介系詞後面接名詞或 -ing。
*   易混副詞：hard 努力／hardly 幾乎不；late 晚／lately 最近；near 靠近／nearly 幾乎；high 講高度／highly 非常；close 靠近／closely 密切、仔細；most 最／mostly 主要是。否定的「也」用 either；already 不放動詞和受詞中間，yet 放問句和否定句句尾。
*   比較級：一個音節加 -er、子音＋y 改 -ier、其他兩個音節以上多半用 more；不規則 good／well→better→best、bad→worse→worst、far→further、little→less→least；more 和 -er 不疊用。further 也是「更多的」（further information）。
*   句型：比較級＋than；the＋最高級＋in（單一地方或團體）／of（一群的複數）；one of the＋最高級＋複數名詞；as＋原級＋as、not as…as；twice as…as；much／far／a lot／even＋比較級（不用 very）；The＋比較級…, the＋比較級…；heavier and heavier。
*   so＋形容詞、such (a)＋形容詞＋名詞、so many／much＋名詞，後面都能接 that 帶出結果；too＋形容詞；形容詞＋enough、enough＋名詞。
