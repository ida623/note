*【點、日、月年】*

## 時間的 at、on、in

**介系詞**（preposition）是放在名詞前面、說明時間、地點、方式這類關係的小字，例如 at、on、in、for、by。時間的 at、on、in 通常這樣分工：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2-grammar/prepositions-of-time-at-in-on)

*   `at`：時刻、用餐時間
*   `on`：星期幾、日期
*   `in`：一天裡的時段，以及月份、季節、年份這類較長的時間

| 介系詞 | 用在 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | 時刻、一天裡的某個點、節日 | `at 9 a.m.`、`at noon`、`at lunchtime`、`at night`、`at the end of the month`、`at Christmas` |
| `on` | 星期幾、日期、某一天的早上下午 | `on Monday`、`on Fridays`、`on 15 March`、`on Monday morning`、`on my birthday` |
| `in` | 一天裡的時段、月、季節、年、世紀 | `in the morning`、`in March`、`in (the) summer`、`in 2026`、`in the 21st century` |
| 不加 | `next`、`last`、`this`、`every`、`tomorrow` 開頭的時間 | `next week`、`last Friday`、`this afternoon`、`every Monday`、`tomorrow morning` |

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/at-on-and-in-time)

不加介系詞的時間詞：

*   each、every、next、last、some、this、that、one、any、all 開頭的時間詞前面，通常不加 at、on、in。
*   ✗ `Are you working on next week?` → ✓ `Are you working next week?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/next)
*   「星期幾＋afternoon」用 on：`I'm going to the dentist on Tuesday afternoon.`；`this afternoon`、`tomorrow afternoon` 前面不加：✗ `at tomorrow afternoon`、✗ `on this afternoon`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-afternoon)

#### 四個要小心的地方

*   **in the morning，但 on Monday morning**：morning、afternoon、evening、night 一般用 in，講某一個特定的早上、下午時用 on：`on the morning of the ninth of November`。
*   **at night 對 in the night**：at night 是泛指晚上；in the night 通常指某一個晚上裡的某個時候。
*   **at the end of 對 in the end**：講某件事結束的那個時間點用 `at the end (of)`；`in the end` 是「最後、終於」：✗ `In the end of the film` → ✓ `At the end of the film`。
*   **in＋一段時間＝從現在算起多久之後：**`in three days' time`、`in three days`；也用來說做一件事花了多久：`He could learn a song in about five minutes.`。

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/at-on-and-in-time)

週末的說法英美不同：英式說 `at the weekend`，美式比較常說 `on the weekend`、`on weekends`。兩種都對。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2-grammar/prepositions-of-time-at-in-on)

*   `The board meeting starts at 9:30 a.m.`（董事會上午九點半開始。）
*   `Our new branch will open on April 1.`（新分店四月一日開幕。）
*   `The company was founded in 1998.`（公司成立於 1998 年。）
*   `Most customers visit the store in the afternoon.`（大多數顧客下午來店。）
*   `The client will call us on Friday morning.`（客戶週五早上會打電話來。）
*   `The office is closed on Sundays.`（辦公室週日休息。）
*   `We will announce the results next week.`（我們下週公布結果。）不加介系詞。
*   `Invoices are sent at the end of each month.`（發票在每個月底寄出。）
*   `The new factory will be ready in two months.`（新工廠兩個月後完工。）

## by 對 until、for 對 during、in 對 within，以及 since、from、before、prior to

by 與 until、since 與 for 配完成式的用法見〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉。下表比較表示期限、期間、起點的介系詞：

| 介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `by` | 最晚在那時之前（期限） | `Please submit the report by Friday.` |
| `until`／`till` | 一直持續到那時 | `The store is open until 10 p.m.` |
| `before` | 比那時早 | `Please call me before 5 p.m.` |
| `after` | 比那時晚 | `The shop reopens after the holiday.` |
| `prior to` | 在……之前 | `Please arrive 30 minutes prior to departure.` |
| `for`＋一段時間 | 持續多久 | `The system was down for three hours.` |
| `during`＋一件事或一段期間 | 在那段時間裡的某個時候 | `Two people left during the presentation.` |
| `throughout` | 整段期間從頭到尾 | `Prices stayed stable throughout the year.` |
| `within` | 在……之內、不超過 | `You will receive a reply within 24 hours.` |
| `in`＋一段時間 | 從現在算起多久之後 | `We're moving to the new office in two months.` |
| `since` | 從過去某時一直到現在 | `She has worked here since 2019.` |
| `from…to`／`from…until` | 從……到…… | `The museum is open from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.` |

*   **by 對 until**：某件事會在某個時間或期限之前發生，用 by，不用 until；until 是「直到」。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/until)

    *   ✗ `The movie will be finished until 9 pm.` → ✓ `The movie will be finished by 9 pm.`
    *   by 是「不晚於」，用在約定和期限。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/by)

*   **by、before、until 三個比：**[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/before)

    *   before：比那時早，那時一到事情一定已經完成
    *   by：那時是最後能完成的時間：`I’ll call you by five o’clock.`（最晚五點）
    *   until：持續到那時為止：`I’ll be there until five o’clock.`（待到五點）

*   **for 對 during**：during 說某件事在一段時間之中的什麼時候發生；for 說某件事持續多久。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/during-or-for)

    *   ✗ `during three and a half hours` → ✓ `for three and a half hours`
    *   解題建議：空格後面是**數字＋時間**（three hours、two weeks）用 for；後面是**一件事的名稱**（the meeting、the presentation）多半用 during。
    *   for 也能接 the winter 這種期間：`They went to Florida for the winter.` 是「整個冬天都在那裡」，跟 during the winter（冬天裡的某個時候）意思不同。

*   **in 對 within**：意思不一樣，within 強調不超過某個範圍，或不晚於某個時間：`They should arrive within three days.`（最晚三天內）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/within)

    *   `We’re going to Cape Town in two months.` 是「兩個月後出發」，跟 for two months（待兩個月）不同。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/for)

*   **after**：意思是「比……晚」和「在時間或位置上的下一個」，後面可以接名詞片語：`Shall we have a swim after lunch?`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/after)

*   **since**：指回過去的某個時間點，後面接日期、時間或名詞片語，主要子句常用現在完成式。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/since)

*   **from…to／until**：from 表示事情開始的時間，`from … to` 講範圍：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/from)

    *   `Sir Edward Youde was the Governor of Hong Kong from 1982 to 1985.`
    *   from 也可以配 until：`The road outside our house will be closed from 6 am until 6 pm tomorrow.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/until)
    *   美式英文也說 `Monday through Friday`（英式偏好 from Monday to Friday）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/across)

*   **prior to、throughout：**

    *   prior to：在某個時間或事件之前：`I was trying to finish up my work prior to the trip.`。to 是介系詞，後面接名詞或 -ing（例如 `prior to shooting`）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/prior-to)
    *   throughout：在每個部分，或整段時間。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/throughout)

> 期限用 by，持續到某時用 until；數字＋時間用 for，事件名稱用 during；最晚多久內用 within。

## 地點的介系詞：at、on、in 與 above、under、between、across……

#### at、on、in

地點的 at、on、in 這樣分：

*   `at`：把位置看成一個點，用在做特定活動的地方、地址或確切的位置：`I was sitting at my desk.`
*   `on`：在一個表面上，也用在樓層、街道、河流與部分交通工具（搭乘中的大眾運輸）：`They live on the 15th floor!`、`I was on the train`
*   `in`：在一個較大的空間或範圍裡面，例如箱子、房子、城市、國家

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2/prepositions-place)、[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/at-on-and-in-place)

| 介系詞 | 看成 | 職場例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `at` | 一個點、一個活動的地方、地址 | `at the reception desk`、`at the airport`、`at a conference`、`at 25 Park Road` |
| `on` | 一個面、一條線、樓層、搭乘中的車船飛機 | `on the fifth floor`、`on the wall`、`on Main Street`、`on the train` |
| `in` | 一個範圍或空間的裡面 | `in the meeting room`、`in the drawer`、`in Taipei`、`in Japan` |

同一個地方有時 at、in 都對，意思稍有不同：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/at-on-and-in-place)

*   公司、工作場所看成做事的地方用 at：`at a software company`
*   看成實體的位置用 in：`in an open-plan office`
*   同理，`at the meeting` 和 `in the meeting` 兩種都聽得到。

#### 上下、之間、對面、穿過

| 介系詞 | 意思與重點 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `above`／`over` | 在……上方。溫度相對零度用 above；數字（超過多少）通常用 over；有接觸、蓋住用 over | `three degrees above zero`、`over 100 people` |
| `below`／`under` | 在……下方。年齡、時間與重量用 under；溫度、高度用 below | `children under 12`、`below zero` |
| `between` | 在兩者之間；也連接兩個時間或數字 | `between the bank and the post office`、`between 9 and 11 a.m.` |
| `among` | 在一群之中 | `among the files on his desk` |
| `next to` | 緊鄰、旁邊 | `The printer is next to the window.` |
| `opposite` | 在對面（面對面、隔著東西） | `The pharmacy is opposite the bank.` |
| `across` | 從一邊到另一邊、在另一邊；遍及各地 | `a bridge across the river`、`stores across the country` |
| `along` | 沿著（又長又細的東西） | `shops along the main street` |
| `through` | 穿過（在某個東西裡面通過） | `drive through the tunnel` |

*   **above／over：**[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/above-or-over)

    *   數字通常用 over，不用 above：✗ `I get above sixty emails a day.` → ✓ `I get over sixty emails a day.`
    *   講相對於零度或平均的溫度，用 above 不用 over。

*   **under／below：**

    *   年齡用 under，不用 below；時間與重量也用 under；高度與溫度用 below。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/under)
    *   講數字、數量、統計偏低時，below 比 under 常用。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/below)

*   **between／among**：between 指兩樣分得很清楚的東西，among 指一群、一堆裡面分不開的東西。時間和數字只能用 between：✗ `among 1998 and 2004` → ✓ `between 1998 and 2004`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/between-or-among)

*   **next to**：兩個人或東西很靠近、中間沒有別的東西。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/next-to)

*   **opposite 對 in front of**：opposite 是面對著、但在另一邊；in front of 是靠近……的前方。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/opposite)

*   **across、through：**[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/across)

    *   across：在某個有邊界的東西（城市、道路、河流）的另一邊，或從一邊到另一邊
    *   across 也用來強調同時在許多地方：`She’s opened coffee shops across the city`
    *   在某個東西「裡面」從一邊到另一邊（森林、長草）用 through，不用 across

*   **along**：沿著又長又細的東西（道路、小徑）排成一線：`There were lots of shops along the main street.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/along)

*   `Please wait at the reception desk.`（請在櫃台等候。）
*   `Our office is on the fifth floor.`（我們辦公室在五樓。）
*   `The company's headquarters is in Singapore.`（公司總部在新加坡。）
*   `Over 200 people attended the job fair.`（超過兩百人參加了就業博覽會。）

*   `Visitors under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.`（未滿十八歲的訪客須由成人陪同。）

*   `The bank is opposite the train station.`（銀行在火車站對面。）
*   `We have 40 stores across the country.`（我們全國有四十家店。）

*【方法、原因、讓步、取代】*

## by、with、without、according to、due to、despite、instead of……

#### by 與 with：怎麼做、用什麼做

*   **by＋交通、通訊、付款方式（不加冠詞）**：用 by＋名詞說怎麼移動、怎麼聯絡、怎麼處理：`I’ll send it by email`、`Can I pay by credit card?`。說 `by car`、`by train`，但名詞前面有 a、the、my 這類字時改用 in 或 on：`Barbara is travelling in Ann’s car.`；走路是 `on foot`，不說 by foot。
*   **by＋-ing**：用什麼方法做到：`By pressing this button, you turn on the alarm system.`。
*   **by 對 with**：講「動作」用 by（`by breaking the window`）；講「用來做事的東西或工具」用 with（`He broke the window with a rock.`）。
*   **by＋數字**：講增加或減少的幅度：`The price of fuel has increased by 12% this year.`。

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/by)

*   with 也有「用（工具）」「和……一起」「有（某個特徵）」的意思：`They opened the package with a knife.`、`It’s the house with the really big gates.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/with)
*   without 是「沒有」，後面接單數可數名詞時要加 a／an：`Don’t go out without a hat.`；without＋-ing 是「沒做……」。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/without)

#### according to、due to／because of、despite／in spite of

*   **according to：**「根據……的說法」，引的是別人或別處的意見，常用在正式場合引報告、統計：`According to a recent report by the Department of Health…`。講自己的意見不能這樣用：✗ `according to me` → ✓ `in my opinion`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/according-to)

*   **because of、due to＋名詞；because＋子句：**

    *   because 是連接詞，帶出原因的子句；because of 是兩個字的介系詞，意思是「由於……的結果」：`Because of the rain, the tennis match was stopped.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/because-because-of)
    *   due to something 也是「因為某件事」：`The bus was delayed due to heavy snow.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/due-to)
    *   because 這類連接詞怎麼接子句，在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉。

*   **despite、in spite of＋名詞或 -ing；although＋子句：**

    *   in spite of 和 despite 後面接名詞、動名詞（-ing）或代名詞；although 和 even though 後面接主詞和動詞。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/contrasting-ideas-although-despite-others)
    *   despite 後面永遠不加 of：✗ `despite of the long queues` → ✓ `despite the long queues`。要接子句就用 `despite the fact that`、`in spite of the fact that`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/despite-and-in-spite-of)

> 空格後面是名詞或 -ing：because of、due to、despite、in spite of。空格後面是主詞＋動詞：because、although。

#### instead of、in addition to、regarding

*   `instead of`：「取代……、而不是……」，後面可以接 -ing：`Instead of complaining, why don't we try to change things?`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/instead-of)
*   `in addition to`：「除了……之外還有」，to 是介系詞，接名詞或 -ing：`She's a fitness instructor in addition to being a full-time student.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/in-addition-to)
*   `regarding`：「關於」，後面直接接名詞，從來不接 to：`I am writing regarding your letter of 31 May.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-regarding) 它和 including、following 這類字在〈分詞與分詞構句〉也出現過。

*   `I'll send you the revised contract by email this afternoon.`（今天下午我用電子郵件寄修改後的合約給你。）
*   `Most employees come to work by train or on foot.`（大多數員工搭火車或走路上班。）
*   `You can save money by booking early.`（提早訂可以省錢。）
*   `According to the latest survey, most customers prefer online ordering.`（根據最新調查，大多數顧客偏好線上訂購。）
*   `The flight was delayed due to heavy fog.`（班機因濃霧延誤。）
*   `Despite the bad weather, the shipment arrived on time.`（雖然天氣很差，貨還是準時到了。）
*   `Although the store was busy, the staff stayed calm.`（店裡雖然很忙，員工還是很冷靜。）
*   `In addition to his regular duties, Mr. Ng trains new staff.`（吳先生除了本來的工作，還負責訓練新人。）

*【動詞、名詞後面跟哪個字】*

## 動詞＋介系詞、名詞＋介系詞：apply for、comply with、increase in…

介系詞跟著哪個字，要一起記：

*   **動詞＋介系詞**：動詞後面接哪個介系詞沒有文法規則可循，最好一起記。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/verbs-prepositions)
*   **名詞＋介系詞**：很多名詞後面習慣接特定的介系詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/nouns-and-prepositions)
*   **形容詞＋介系詞**：responsible for、familiar with……在〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉。
*   **介系詞＋動詞**：介系詞後面的動詞用 -ing：✗ `decided on to do` → ✓ `She’s decided on doing a Chinese language course.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositional-phrases) to 剛好是介系詞的那幾個（look forward to）在〈不定詞與動名詞〉。

| 動詞＋介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apply for` | 申請（工作、補助） | `She applied for the sales manager position.` |
| `comply with` | 遵守（規定） | `All factories must comply with the new regulations.` |
| `depend on`、`rely on`、`concentrate on` | 取決於、依賴、專心於 | `The price depends on the number of guests.` |
| `respond to`、`refer to` | 回覆、參考 | `Please respond to this email by Friday.` |
| `provide` 人 `with` 東西、`deal with` | 提供、處理 | `We will provide you with a laptop.` |
| `specialize in`、`succeed in` | 專門做、成功 | `Our firm specializes in tax law.` |
| `participate in` | 參加 | `Over 300 companies participated in the trade fair.` |
| `register for` | 報名 | `Please register for the workshop by May 10.` |
| `subscribe to` | 訂閱 | `We subscribe to two industry magazines.` |

| 名詞＋介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `increase in`／`increase of` | in 接「增加的東西」，of 接「增加的幅度」 | `an increase in profits`、`a sales increase of 30%` |
| `demand for` | 對……的需求 | `There is strong demand for electric cars.` |
| `access to` | 使用、進入……的權限 | `Only managers have access to the payroll files.` |
| `reason for` | ……的原因 | `What was the reason for the delay?` |
| `solution to` | ……的解決辦法 | `We need a solution to the parking problem.` |
| `need for` | 對……的需要 | `There is a need for more training.` |
| `damage to` | 對……的損害 | `The storm caused damage to the roof.` |
| `delay in` | ……的延誤 | `We apologize for the delay in shipping.` |
| `response to`、`interest in`、`knowledge of` | 對……的回應、對……的興趣、對……的了解 | `This job requires knowledge of Spanish.` |

出處：劍橋辭典 [apply](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/apply)、[comply](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/comply)、[participate](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/participate)、[register](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/register)、[subscribe](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/subscribe)、[demand](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/demand)、[access](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/access)、[damage](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/damage)、[delay](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/delay)；[英國文化協會（動詞＋介系詞）](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/verbs-prepositions)；劍橋文法 [increase](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-increase-in-or-increase-of)

名詞後面的介系詞，常見的錯法：

*   ✗ `reason of` → ✓ `reason for`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-reason)
*   ✗ `solution of／for something` → ✓ `solution to something`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-solution)
*   ✗ `a／the need of something` → ✓ `a／the need for something`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-need)
*   approach、reaction、response 後面接 to；knowledge 後面接 of。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/nouns-and-prepositions)

多益的 `increase` 題要看後面接什麼：

*   **in**：後面是**漲的東西**（profits、sales、the number of…）用 in。
*   **of**：後面是**漲了多少**（30%、$10,000）用 of。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-increase-in-or-increase-of)

同理，`decrease`、`rise` 也這樣用：

*   `a decrease in something`、`a decrease of two percent`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-decrease)
*   `a rise in something`、`a rise of two percent`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/prepositions-rise)

## 綜合練習：照多益第五部分的樣子

介系詞題先看空格後面：是時間（點、日、月、一段時間）、地點、名詞還是子句；再看前面的動詞、名詞、形容詞有沒有固定搭配。

**🧩 小練習：** The shareholders' meeting will take place ______ Thursday, May 14, at the Grand Hotel.

- (A) in
- (B) at
- (C) by
- (D) on

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(D)** 整句：股東會將於 5 月 14 日星期四在 Grand Hotel 舉行。  
星期幾、日期用 on。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** Thank you ______ your patience while we update our system.

- (A) to
- (B) for
- (C) of
- (D) about

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(B)** 整句：感謝您在我們更新系統期間耐心等候。  
感謝某件事：thank you for，例如 `He thanked me for the flowers.`。

</details>

### 重點整理

*   時間：at＋時刻（at 9 a.m.、at night、at the end of）；on＋星期幾、日期、特定某天的早上（on Monday morning）；in＋時段、月、季、年（in the morning、in March、in 2026）；next、last、this、every、tomorrow 前面不加介系詞。
*   期限與期間：by＝最晚那時；until＝一直到那時；for＋數字時間（for three hours）；during＋事件或期間（during the meeting）；within＝不超過；in＋一段時間＝多久之後；since＋過去時間點（配完成式）；prior to＝before；throughout＝整段期間。
*   地點：at 點、on 面與樓層、in 範圍；溫度 above zero、數字 over 100；年齡 under 18；between 兩者、among 一群；opposite 對面；across 另一邊或遍及；along 沿著；through 從裡面穿過。
*   by email、by car，但 in my car、on foot；by＋-ing 是方法、with＋工具；without＋a＋單數名詞。according to 引別人的說法。
*   後面接名詞或 -ing：because of、due to、despite、in spite of（despite 不加 of）、instead of、in addition to、regarding（不加 to）；後面接子句：because、although。
*   固定搭配：apply for、comply with、depend on、respond to、participate in、provide 人 with 東西；increase in（漲的東西）／increase of（漲幅）、demand for、access to、reason for、solution to、need for、damage to。
