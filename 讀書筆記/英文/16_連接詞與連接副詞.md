## 對等連接詞：and、but、or、so、yet、nor

**連接詞**（conjunction）是把字、片語或子句接起來的字。〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉講過：同一個主詞做兩個動作時，兩個有時態的動詞中間要有 and、but 這類連接詞。兩個**子句**（各有自己主詞和動詞的一組字）要接成一句，也要靠連接詞（或分號，見本課最後一段）。

第一種是**對等連接詞**（coordinating conjunction），連接文法上同一類的東西，例如字、片語、子句；最常見的是 and、or、but。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions)

*   **一共七個**：and、but、for、or、nor、so、yet，放在獨立子句開頭，把它接到前一個子句上。[出處](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/independent_and_dependent_clauses/index.html)
*   **口訣 FANBOYS**：照 for、and、nor、but、or、yet、so 的順序排，第一個字母連起來是 FANBOYS。[出處](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/fragments-and-run-ons/)

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `and` | 和、而且 | `Ms. Lin finished the report and sent it to the manager.` |
| `but` | 但是（前後意思相反） | `The meeting was long, but it was very useful.` |
| `or` | 或者 | `You can pay by credit card or by bank transfer.` |
| `so` | 所以（後面接結果） | `The client approved the budget, so we can start next week.` |
| `yet` | 但是、然而 | `The software is expensive, yet thousands of small firms use it.` |
| `nor` | 也不（接在否定句後面） | `We didn't see the factory, nor did we meet the manager.` |
| `for` | 因為 | `…hummus, for it is very garlicky.` |

*   **yet**：當連接詞時意思是 but 或 nevertheless，表示對比。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/yet)
*   **nor**：接在否定子句後面時，nor 後面的主詞和動詞要倒過來：`We didn’t get to see the castle, nor did we see the cathedral.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/neither-neither-nor-and-not-either) 倒裝的完整說明在〈倒裝、省略與多益文法題解題順序〉那課。

#### 四個要注意的地方

*   **逗號**：and、or、but 連接兩個主要子句（能自己成句的子句）時，主詞相同通常不加逗號，主詞不同有時會加。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/punctuation) 也有寫作指南要求：第二個完整子句用對等連接詞開頭時，連接詞前面要加逗號。[出處](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/independent_and_dependent_clauses/index.html) 兩種說法不完全一樣；自己寫信時，兩個主詞不同的子句之間加逗號比較清楚。
*   **表示結果的 so 子句一定放後面**：用 so 子句連接原因和結果時，so 子句一定在第二個。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-causes-reasons-results-and-purpose)
*   **否定句裡用 or，不用 and**：動詞的否定形後面用 or：`I don’t like tea or coffee.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/or)
*   **一次只用一個連接詞**：除了兩個字一組的連接詞，連接時只用一個連接詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions) 中文說「因為……所以……」、「雖然……但是……」，英文只能挑一個：  
    ✗ `Because my alarm didn’t go off, so I was late.` → ✓ `Because my alarm didn’t go off, I was late for work.`

**🧩 小練習：** The hotel near the airport is expensive, ______ the rooms are very small.

- (A) so
- (B) or
- (C) for
- (D) but

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(D)** 整句：機場附近那家飯店很貴，但房間很小。  
價錢貴、房間卻小，跟預期相反，用 but。so 會變成「因為貴所以房間小」；for 會變成「因為房間小所以貴」；or 是二選一，都不通。

</details>

- (A) So
- (B) But

**🧩 小練習：** 下面哪一句正確？

- (A) Because the printer was broken, so we sent the files to a copy shop.
- (B) The printer was broken, we sent the files to a copy shop.
- (C) Because the printer was broken, we sent the files to a copy shop.
- (D) So we sent the files to a copy shop, the printer was broken.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(C)** Because the printer was broken, we sent… 只用一個連接詞，正確。Because…, so… 用了兩個連接詞；The printer was broken, we sent… 兩個完整句子之間只有逗號；So we sent…, the printer was broken 把 so 子句放到前面，都錯。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** The manager did not approve the budget, ______ did she explain why.

- (A) or
- (B) so
- (C) and
- (D) nor

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(D)** 整句：經理沒有核准預算，也沒有說明原因。  
前面是否定句，後面接「也沒有解釋」，而且 did she 已經倒裝，用 nor。or、and、so 後面不倒裝；so did she 是「她也一樣」，意思也不對。

</details>

## 相關連接詞：both…and、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also、whether…or

**相關連接詞**（correlative conjunction）是兩個字一組、前後呼應的連接詞，有些書把它們算成對等連接詞的一種（有兩個部分的對等連接詞）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions) 前後兩半接的東西要同一類，例如字、片語、子句。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/either-or)

| 相關連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `both A and B` | A 和 B 兩個都 | `Both the sales team and the design team will attend.` |
| `either A or B` | 不是 A 就是 B，二選一 | `You can reach us either by phone or by email.` |
| `neither A nor B` | A 和 B 都不 | `Neither the hotel nor the airline offered a refund.` |
| `not only A but (also) B` | 不只 A，還有 B | `The new system is not only faster but also cheaper.` |
| `whether A or B` | 是 A 還是 B；不管 A 還是 B | `We haven't decided whether to hire a designer or train someone.` |

*   **both…and**：強調兩件事的連結，比單用 and 更強。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/both)
*   **neither…nor**：是 either…or 的否定形：`Neither Joe nor Maria will be at the wedding.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/or)
*   **not only…but also**：用在正式的場合，also 有時可以省略。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/not-only-but-also) Not only 放句首時要倒裝，在〈倒裝、省略與多益文法題解題順序〉那課講。
*   **whether…or**：帶出一個有兩個選項的子句，另有兩條限制：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whether)
*   後面接 to＋原形時只能用 whether，不能用 if。
*   whether…or not 可以表示「不管……都」，這時也不能換成 either。

當主詞時，動詞的單複數：

*   **both 帶的名詞**接複數動詞，**either、neither 帶的名詞**接單數動詞。這條講的是 either、neither 單獨當數量詞用的時候（例如 either option、neither of the supermarkets），不是 either A or B。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/quantifiers)
*   **either…or、neither…nor 一單一複**：動詞跟最靠近的名詞一致；建議把複數放在靠近動詞的位置、用複數動詞。[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/connected-singular-and-plural-noun-phrases-1/) 完整說明在〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉那課。

## 從屬連接詞與時間：when、while、before、until、as soon as、since

第二種是**從屬連接詞**（subordinating conjunction），常見的有 after、(al)though、as、before、if、since、that、until、when、whereas、while、once、as soon as、provided that。這些連接詞後面接子句時，這個子句就變成**從屬子句**：不能自己成句，要掛在**主要子句**上才是完整的句子。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions)

so 的歸類有兩種：有些清單把它列成從屬連接詞，上面那份七個對等連接詞的清單則把表示結果的 so 算成對等連接詞。

*   **逗號**：從屬子句在主要子句前面時，通常在從屬子句結尾加逗號；主要子句在前面時，不需要逗號。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions)
*   **位置固定在子句開頭**：從屬連接詞一定在子句的開頭，整個從屬子句可以搬到主要子句前面或後面。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions)

#### 時間連接詞

when、after、before、until、since、while、once、as、as soon as 都是從屬連接詞，把一個動作或事件連到一個時間點。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-time)

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `when` | ……的時候 | `When the client arrives, please call me.` |
| `while` | 在……的期間（同時進行） | `The phone rang while we were having lunch.` |
| `before`／`after` | 在……之前／之後 | `Please check the figures before you send the report.` |
| `until` | 一直到…… | `Please wait here until Ms. Chen comes to meet you.` |
| `as soon as`／`once` | 一……就…… | `We will ship the order as soon as we receive payment.` |
| `since` | 自從……以來 | `Mr. Park has worked in Tokyo since the office opened.` |

*   **while**：表示動作或事件同時發生，過去、現在、未來都可以用。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-time)
*   **since＋主要子句用現在完成式**：since 指過去某個時間點時，since 後面可以用過去簡單式或現在完成式，主要子句用現在完成式：`They haven’t received any junk mail since they moved house.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/since)
*   **講未來不用 will**：指未來時間的連接詞後面不用 will：  
    ✗ `I will call you as soon as I will get to the office.` → ✓ `I will call you as soon as I get to the office.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-time) 完整說明在〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉那課。

## 原因與讓步：because、since、as；although、even though、whereas

#### 表示原因：because、since、as

as、because、since 都帶出從屬子句，把結果和原因連起來。三個意思很接近，差別在語氣：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/as-because-or-since)

*   **because**：比 as 和 since 常見。
*   **as、since**：比 because 正式。

三個意思一樣。

*   **since 有兩個意思：**「自從」（時間）和「因為」（原因）。當「因為」用時，since 帶出從屬子句說明原因：`They couldn’t deliver the parcel since no one was there to answer the door.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/since)
*   **because 子句不能自己成句**：口語可以單獨回答 Because…，但正式寫作不單獨使用 because 子句。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/as-because-or-since)

#### 表示讓步：although、though、even though

**讓步**是「雖然 A，還是 B」：A 本來會讓人以為 B 不會發生。

*   **although、though**：都是「儘管」的意思，都是從屬連接詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/although-or-though)
*   **even though**：比 although 稍微強一點、更強調。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/contrasting-ideas-although-despite-others)

兩個要注意的地方：

*   **although 和 but 不能一起用：**✗ `But I’m tall, I’m not as tall as Bill.` → ✓ `Although I’m tall, I’m not as tall as Bill.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting) 前面說過，一次只用一個連接詞，所以也不說 Although…, but…。
*   **even if＝不管是不是**：even if 的意思是 whether or not：`Even if you run, you’ll still be late.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting)

#### 對比兩件事：whereas、while

*   **whereas**：表示兩件事實或想法之間的對比。在對比的句子裡跟 while 一樣，但 while 指時間時不能換成 whereas。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whereas)
*   **while**：最常用來講時間，但也可以表示「儘管」（＝although）。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/contrasting-ideas)

例句：

*   `Because the train was delayed, Mr. Ota was late for the interview.`（因為火車誤點，太田先生面試遲到了。）
*   `As everyone already knows each other, we can skip the introductions.`（大家都認識了，自我介紹就省略。）
*   `Although the budget was small, the campaign was a success.`（雖然預算不多，宣傳活動很成功。）
*   `The project was finished on time even though two members were on leave.`（雖然有兩個人在休假，專案還是準時完成。）
*   `Sales in Asia rose, whereas sales in Europe fell slightly.`（亞洲的銷售成長，歐洲則小幅下滑。）

## 條件、目的與結果：unless、as long as、in case；so that；so…that、such…that

#### 條件

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `if` | 如果 | `If you have any questions, please call me.` |
| `unless` | 除非（＝if…not） | `We cannot ship the order unless we receive payment.` |
| `provided (that)`、`as long as` | 只要（附帶條件） | `You may work from home as long as you finish your tasks on time.` |
| `in case` | 以防萬一 | `Take some extra brochures in case more visitors come.` |

*   **unless**：意思是「除非」，跟 if…not 一樣。它跟 if 一樣是條件詞，所以 unless 子句裡不用 will 或 would。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/unless)
*   **as long as、provided that**：用來加上特定的條件或設限制，例子有 as long as、provided (that)、on condition that；provided (that) 比較正式、多用在書面。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conditionals-other-expressions-unless-should-as-long-as)
*   **in case 不是 if**：in case 講的是為了可能發生的情況先做準備，不當 if 用。另外 in case of 是介系詞，後面接名詞：`In case of breakdown, please press the alarm button…`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/in-case-of)

#### 目的：so that

*   so that 和 in order that 用來談目的，常和 can、would、will 這類助動詞一起用。
*   so that 比 in order that 常見得多，in order that 比較正式。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/so-that-or-in-order-that)
*   so that 後面接子句；in order to 後面接原形動詞：`You have to look at the picture really carefully in order to see all the detail.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

#### 結果：so＋形容詞／副詞＋that，such＋名詞片語＋that

such 與 so 的分工：

*   **接什麼**：such 接名詞片語，so 接形容詞或副詞；much、many、little、few 前面用 so，不用 such。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/such-or-so)
*   **加 that 子句**：後面加 that 子句說出結果，這個句型裡不用 very：`It was so hot that we didn’t leave the air-conditioned room all day.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/so)
*   **例句**：`He was such a good teacher that we all passed the exam.`、`There's so much noise that I can't think!`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/intensifiers-so-such)

> so＋形容詞（so busy）；such＋a＋形容詞＋名詞（such a busy week）；so many／so much＋名詞。

## 連接詞對介系詞：because 對 because of、although 對 despite、while 對 during

中文都翻成「因為」「雖然」「在……期間」，英文卻分兩種：**連接詞**後面接子句（有主詞、有時態的動詞），**介系詞**後面接名詞、代名詞或 -ing。介系詞本身的用法在〈介系詞〉那課。

| 意思 | 連接詞＋子句 | 介系詞＋名詞或 -ing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 因為 | `because`、`since`、`as` | `because of`、`due to`、`owing to` |
| 雖然、儘管 | `although`、`though`、`even though` | `despite`、`in spite of` |
| 在……期間 | `while` | `during` |
| 萬一 | `in case` | `in case of` |

每一列的用法：

*   **because 對 because of**：because 是連接詞，用在有動詞的子句前面；because of 是介系詞，一定用在名詞或名詞片語前面：`I was late because there was heavy traffic.`、`I was late because of heavy traffic.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-patterns-because-or-because-of) due to 和 owing to 也是 because of 的意思：`The bus was delayed due to heavy snow.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/due-to)、`The concert has been cancelled owing to lack of interest.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/owing-to)
*   **although 對 despite**：in spite of 和 despite 後面接名詞、動名詞（動詞的 -ing 形）或代名詞；although 和 even though 後面接主詞和動詞。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/contrasting-ideas-although-despite-others) despite 後面永遠不加 of（✗ `despite of`），in spite of 是分開的三個字，也不直接接 that 子句，要接子句就寫 in spite of the fact that、despite the fact that。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/in-spite-of-and-despite)
*   **while 對 during**：during 是介系詞，不是連接詞，所以後面一定接名詞或代名詞：  
    ✗ `She studies during she is on the train.` → ✓ `She studies while she is on the train.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting)
*   **in case 對 in case of**：in case 是連接詞或副詞，in case of 是介系詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/in-case-of)

> 看空格後面：有主詞＋有時態的動詞 → 連接詞；只有名詞片語 → 介系詞。

-ing 要多看一眼：despite、in spite of 後面可以接 -ing（`They arrived late despite leaving in plenty of time.`）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/in-spite-of-and-despite)，但 although、though 在正式寫作也能接 -ing 或省略動詞的子句（`Though more expensive, the new model is safer and more efficient.`）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/although-or-though)，while、when 接 -ing 的寫法在〈分詞與分詞構句〉講過。所以空格後面是 -ing 時，不能只靠「名詞還是子句」判斷，要再看意思和其他選項。

例句：

*   `The reception was moved indoors because of the heavy rain.`／`…because it was raining heavily.`（因為下大雨，酒會移到室內。）
*   `Despite the high price, the new model sold well.`／`Although the price was high, …`（雖然價格高，新機型還是賣得很好。）
*   `Please keep your phone on silent during the presentation.`／`…while Mr. Lee is presenting.`（簡報時請把手機調成靜音。）
*   `The shipment was delayed due to a problem at the port.`（因為港口出問題，貨晚到了。）
*   `Despite the fact that sales fell, the company opened two new stores.`（雖然業績下滑，公司還是開了兩家新店。）

*【看起來像連接詞的副詞】*

## 連接副詞：however、therefore、moreover，以及標點

**連接副詞**是 however、therefore、moreover 這類「把兩句的意思接起來，但文法上是副詞」的字。意思上跟連接詞類似，文法上不一樣：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions)

*   **連接副詞**（however、therefore、as a result 這類）：是副詞片語，可以放在副詞能放的任何位置。
*   **從屬連接詞**：不能隨便換位置，而且它在兩個子句之間建立文法上的連結。

| 意思 | 連接副詞 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 但是、不過 | `however` | `This is one possible solution to the problem. However, there are others.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/however) |
| 儘管如此 | `nevertheless` | `Nevertheless, accidents still occur.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/nevertheless) |
| 因此 | `therefore`、`consequently` | `The company is trying to reduce its costs; consequently, staff who leave are not being replaced.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/consequently) |
| 此外、而且 | `moreover`、`furthermore`、`in addition` | `The whole report is badly written. Moreover, it's inaccurate.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/moreover) |
| 不然、否則 | `otherwise` | `I'd better write it down, otherwise I'll forget it.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/otherwise) |

#### 標點：三種寫法

*   **句號後面開新句：**`The supplier raised its prices. However, the quality is still good.`
*   **分號後面接**：第二個完整子句用 however 這類字開頭時，前面要用分號：`Jim studied in the Sweet Shop for his chemistry quiz; however, it was hard to concentrate because of the noise.` 這類字常見的有 also、consequently、furthermore、however、moreover、nevertheless、therefore。[出處](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/independent_and_dependent_clauses/index.html) 分號也可以代替句號，把兩個主要子句分開。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/punctuation)
*   **放在句子中間**：前後各一個逗號：`There may, however, be other reasons that we don't know about.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/however)；也可以放在 and 後面：`We were unable to get funding and therefore had to abandon the project.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/therefore)

#### 不能像連接詞一樣，只用逗號接兩個句子

兩個完整句子之間只放一個逗號，英文叫 comma splice，是錯句。[出處](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/fragments-and-run-ons/) 改法有三種：[出處](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/independent_and_dependent_clauses/index.html)

*   **換成句號**：✗ `The price is high, the quality is good.` → ✓ `The price is high. The quality is good.`
*   **換成分號**：`The price is high; the quality is good.`
*   **加連接詞**：`The price is high, but the quality is good.`

however、therefore 是副詞，不是連接詞：

*   只用逗號接兩個句子，一樣是 comma splice：✗ `The price is high, however the quality is good.`
*   也不能像 but 那樣連接字和片語：✗ `My teacher is very nice however a bit strict.` → ✓ `My teacher is very nice but a bit strict.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/conjunctions-contrasting)

| 要說的意思 | 連接詞（逗號就夠） | 連接副詞（句號或分號） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 但是 | `The price is high, but the quality is good.` | `The price is high; however, the quality is good.` |
| 所以 | `The flight was cancelled, so we stayed another night.` | `The flight was cancelled. Therefore, we stayed another night.` |

*   **otherwise 例外**：字典也把 otherwise 標成連接詞，所以逗號後面可以直接接：`I'd better write it down, otherwise I'll forget it.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/otherwise)
*   **in addition 與 in addition to**：in addition 是連接副詞；in addition to 是介系詞，後面接名詞或 -ing：`In addition to his apartment in Manhattan, he has a villa in Italy…`、`All employees receive paid holiday and sick leave. In addition, we offer a range of benefits for new parents.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/in-addition-to)

### 重點整理

*   對等連接詞（for、and、nor、but、or、yet、so，口訣 FANBOYS）連接同一類的東西；表示結果的 so 子句一定放後面；否定句裡用 or；一句話只用一個連接詞，不說 Because…, so…、Although…, but…。
*   相關連接詞（correlative conjunction）：both…and、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also、whether…or；前後接同一類的東西。whether 後面可以接 to＋原形，if 不行。
*   從屬連接詞帶出的子句不能自己成句，要掛在主要子句上；從屬子句放前面時加逗號。時間：when、while、before、after、until、as soon as、once、since；講未來時，子句用現在式。
*   原因 because、since、as；讓步 although、though、even though（even if＝不管是不是）；對比 whereas、while（while 指時間時不能換成 whereas）；條件 unless（＝if…not）、as long as、provided that、in case；目的 so that＋子句、in order to＋原形；結果 so＋形容詞＋that、such＋名詞片語＋that、so many／much。
*   看空格後面：子句用連接詞（because、although、while、in case），名詞用介系詞（because of、due to、despite、in spite of、during、in case of）；despite 不加 of，要接子句就用 despite the fact that。
*   however、therefore、moreover、nevertheless、consequently 是連接副詞：用句號或分號隔開，後面加逗號，或用兩個逗號夾在句中；只用逗號接兩個句子是錯句（comma splice），要換成 but、so 這類連接詞。
