## 關係代名詞：先行詞是人用 who，是東西用 which，that 兩種都行

**關係子句**（relative clause）是放在名詞後面、說明「是哪一個」或補充資料的子句，可以把兩個子句合成一句，不必重複同一個名詞：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses_2)

*   `The couple posted a Christmas present to their daughter, who lives in South Africa.`：who 代替 their daughter，不必再說一次。

被關係子句說明的那個名詞叫**先行詞**（antecedent，「先出現的那個詞」）；帶出關係子句的 who、which、that 這些字叫**關係代名詞**（relative pronoun）。

> The engineer who designed the warehouse is retiring.：先行詞 engineer，關係代名詞 who，關係子句 who designed the warehouse。

| 關係代名詞 | 先行詞 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `who` | 人 | `We need someone who can speak Korean.` |
| `which` | 東西、動物 | `The printer which is on the second floor needs toner.` |
| `that` | 人或東西（只用在沒有逗號的關係子句） | `Please return the laptop that you borrowed.` |
| `whom` | 人，當受詞，正式 | `the manager to whom I reported` |
| `whose` | 表示「……的」 | `a supplier whose prices are low` |

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/relative-pronouns-relative-clauses)

*   **that 比較不正式**：that 人、東西都能指，但比較不正式，而且只用在限定的關係子句。限定的關係子句就是沒有逗號、用來指出「是哪一個」的那種；有逗號的叫非限定，後面「限定與非限定」那段細講。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)
*   **who 不用在東西**：先行詞是東西，用 which 或 that。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)
*   **代表一群人的名詞**：committee、government、team 這類字常用 who，所以這類先行詞後面 who、which 都可能看到。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

who 和 which 對調就錯：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-which-or-who)

*   ✗ `The restaurant who is next to the pub is good.` → ✓ `The restaurant which is next to the pub is good.`
*   ✗ `The boy which is wearing the red coat is called Paul.` → ✓ `The boy who is wearing the red coat is called Paul.`

關係代名詞已經是子句的主詞或受詞，子句裡不再重複：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses-defining-and-non-defining)

*   ✗ `She’s the lady who she lent me her phone.` → ✓ `She’s the lady who lent me her phone.`
*   ✗ `We had a lovely meal at the place which Phil recommended it.` → ✓ `We had a lovely meal at the place which Phil recommended.`

例句：

*   `The engineer who designed the new warehouse will give a tour on Friday.`（設計新倉庫的工程師週五會帶大家參觀。）
*   `The printer which is on the second floor needs new toner.`（二樓那台印表機要換碳粉了。）
*   `Applicants who have not received an email should contact HR.`（沒收到信的應徵者請聯絡人資部。）
*   `Please return the laptop that you borrowed last week.`（請歸還你上週借的筆電。）
*   `Ms. Ito is the consultant that recommended this software.`（伊藤小姐就是推薦這套軟體的顧問。）

## 當主詞還是當受詞：whom，以及可以省略的關係代名詞

關係代名詞在關係子句裡可以當主詞，也可以當受詞：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses-defining-and-non-defining)

*   `They’re the people who/that bought our house.`：the people 買了房子，當主詞
*   `They’re the people who/that she met at Jon’s party.`：她遇見那些人，當受詞

| 空格後面 | 關係代名詞當 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 直接接動詞 | 主詞（缺主詞） | `the clients who visited our factory` |
| 接主詞＋動詞，動詞少了受詞 | 受詞（缺受詞） | `the report which the manager requested` |
| 接主詞＋動詞＋介系詞，介系詞後面空著 | 介系詞的受詞 | `the file which you were looking for` |

缺受詞時，關係子句裡一定要有主詞：✗ `They met at the gallery that had talked about.` → ✓ `They met at the gallery that Jane had talked about.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses-typical-errors)

#### whom：受格，正式

whom 與 who：

*   **whom**：who 的受格，在正式文體或書面裡指人，而且這個人是動詞的受詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/who-whom)
*   **現在一般用 who**：受詞的位置現在一般用 who，例如 `This is George, who you met at our house last year.`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/relative-pronouns-relative-clauses)

所以在受詞的位置，who、whom、that 都對，例如 `The parents (whom/who/that) we interviewed…`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns) 主詞的位置就只能用 who，不能用 whom。

#### 可以省略的關係代名詞

不正式的文體裡常省略關係代名詞，但有條件：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

*   只有在限定的關係子句裡、關係代名詞是動詞的受詞時，才省略
*   關係代名詞當主詞時不省略
*   非限定的關係子句不省略

好用的判斷法：關係代名詞後面接的是主詞，通常可以省；後面接的是動詞，通常不能省。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/relative-clauses-defining-relative-clauses)

*   `The proposal (that) we discussed yesterday has been approved.`（that 後面接主詞 we，可以省。）
*   `The employee who answered the phone was very polite.`（who 後面接動詞 answered，不能省。）

例句：

*   `The candidate who we interviewed on Monday has accepted the offer.`（週一面試的那位應徵者接受了錄取。）
*   `The report which the manager requested is almost finished.`（經理要的報告快完成了。）
*   `Ms. Diaz is the person whom you should contact about refunds.`（退款的事請聯絡迪亞茲小姐。）
*   `This is the sales representative that we have been working with for ten years.`（這位就是跟我們合作了十年的業務代表。）

## whose＋名詞：表示所有

whose 用在名詞前面，代替 my、your、his、her、its、our、their、x’s 這類所有格：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

*   `He’s marrying a girl whose family don’t seem to like him.`：whose family＝那個女孩的家人

whose 不只用在人，人、動物、東西的所有都能用：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whose)

*   `This is the book whose title I couldn’t remember.`：whose title＝那本書的書名

> whose 後面接名詞（whose prices、whose new office）；whose 代替 my、their 這類所有格，所以那個名詞前面不再加 the 或 a。

*   **whose 對 who’s**：who’s 是 who is，兩個不要搞混。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whose)
*   **whose 對 who、which**：看空格後面接什麼。

| 空格後面 | 填 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 名詞，前面沒有 the、a，而且是先行詞「的」東西 | `whose` | `a supplier whose prices`：供應商的價格 |
| 主詞（the、a 開頭的名詞，或 we、Jane 這類）＋動詞，句子缺受詞 | `who`、`whom`、`which`、`that` | `the report which the manager requested`：經理不是報告的 |
| 直接接動詞 | `who`、`which`、`that` | `the clients who visited our factory` |

例句：

*   `We are looking for a supplier whose prices are competitive.`（我們在找價格有競爭力的供應商。）
*   `Mr. Lin, whose team won the sales award, will speak at the dinner.`（林先生的團隊拿到業績獎，他會在晚宴上致詞。）
*   `Customers whose orders were delayed will receive a discount coupon.`（訂單延誤的顧客會收到折價券。）
*   `We visited a factory whose machines run 24 hours a day.`（我們參觀了一間機器 24 小時運轉的工廠。）

## 限定與非限定的關係子句；which 指前面整句話

|   | 限定的關係子句 | 非限定的關係子句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 做什麼 | 說明「是哪一個」，拿掉就不知道在說誰 | 補充資料，拿掉也知道在說誰 |
| 逗號（書面） | 不加 | 前後加逗號 |
| 能不能用 that | 可以 | 不行 |
| 受詞能不能省略 | 可以 | 不行 |
| 例句 | `The employees who worked overtime received a bonus.` | `Our CEO, who joined in 2015, will retire next year.` |

出處：[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses-defining-and-non-defining)、[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/relative-pronouns-relative-clauses)

#### 同一句話，有逗號、沒逗號意思不同

有逗號、沒逗號，意思不同：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses-defining-and-non-defining)

*   `His brother, who works at the supermarket, is a friend of mine.`：他只有一個哥哥，這個哥哥在超市上班。
*   `His brother who works at the supermarket is a friend of mine.`：他不只一個哥哥，在說的是在超市上班的那一個。

同理：

*   `The employees, who had worked overtime, received a bonus.`：員工都加了班，也都拿到獎金。
*   `The employees who had worked overtime received a bonus.`：只有加班的那些員工拿到獎金。

#### which 指前面整句話

指整句話的關係子句：

*   **一定用 which**：指整個子句或整句話的關係子句，一定用 which 帶出，例如 `There’s going to be a new headteacher in September, which is good.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)
*   **逗號**：書面上 which 前面通常加逗號。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-clauses_2)
*   **不能用 that 或 what**：✗ `…in the two languages, what helps a lot` → ✓ `…in the two languages, which helps a lot`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

#### 數量詞＋of whom／of which

關係代名詞常和數量詞、數字一起用，例如 all of which／whom、most of which／whom、none of which／whom、two of whom：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/relative-pronouns-relative-clauses)

*   `She has three brothers, two of whom are in the army.`
*   `I read three books last week, one of which I really enjoyed.`

指人用 whom，指東西用 which；介系詞後面不直接接 who 或 that（下一段會講）。

*【介系詞放哪裡，以及地點、時間、原因】*

## 介系詞＋whom／which；關係副詞 where、when、why

#### 介系詞放前面或放後面

介系詞可以直接放在關係代名詞前面，也可以放在關係子句的最後：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

| 介系詞位置 | 語氣 | 指東西 | 指人 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 關係代名詞前面 | 比較正式 | `the community in which he grew up` | `There was only one person to whom the old man spoke.` |
| 關係子句最後 | 比較不正式 | `the community which I grew up in` | `…who the old man spoke to.` |

*   **介系詞後面不接 who**：用 who 時，介系詞放在關係子句最後，不直接放在 who 前面。錯句：✗ `the one on who I know I can rely`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)
*   **介系詞後面不接 that**：which、whom、whose 前面可以直接放介系詞，that 不行。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

> 介系詞放前面：to whom（人）、in which（東西）；放後面：who…to、which／that…in。

#### 關係副詞：where、when、why

不正式的用語裡，常用 where、when、why 代替 at which、on which、for which 帶出限定的關係子句：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

*   `I know a restaurant where the food is excellent.`（＝at which）
*   `There isn’t a day when I don’t feel rushed off my feet.`（＝on which）
*   `Do you know the reason why the shop is closed today?`（＝for which）

指時間不用 where：✗ `another date where everyone can attend` → ✓ `another date when everyone can attend`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/word-choice-where-or-when)

#### where 對 which：看子句完不完整

which 加上介系詞也可以指地點和時間，但用 where 或 when 代替 which 加介系詞更常見；which 沒有介系詞時，就不能換成 where 或 when：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/relative-clauses-non-defining-relative-clauses)

*   `City Park, where we used to go, …`（＝which we used to go to）
*   `Centre Park, which we love, …`：which 沒有介系詞，不能換成 where

| 空格後面 | 填 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 完整的子句（不缺主詞、不缺受詞） | `where`／`when` | `the hotel where we stayed`（stay 不接受詞） |
| 缺受詞（動詞或介系詞後面空著） | `which`／`that` | `the hotel which we recommended`（recommend 少了受詞） |

*【前面沒有先行詞的時候】*

## what＝the thing that，不是關係代名詞

what 可以當代名詞，意思是「……的東西」（`the thing(s) that`），但不當關係代名詞：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/what)

*   `What we need to do is make a list of useful phone numbers.`：what＝the thing that
*   ✗ `This is the book what the lecturer mentioned.` → ✓ `This is the book which the lecturer mentioned.`

> 前面有名詞（先行詞）→ which／that；前面沒有名詞 → what（＝the thing that）。

前面有先行詞，用 which 或 that，不用 what，例如 `Another activity which/that I have chosen is photography.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns)

what 帶出的子句當主詞、受詞的完整用法在〈名詞子句〉那課。

例句：

*   `What the client wants is a faster delivery time.`（客戶要的是更快的交期。）
*   `This is not what we ordered.`（這不是我們訂的東西。）
*   `These are the samples that we ordered.`（這些就是我們訂的樣品。）
*   `Everything that you need is in this folder.`（你需要的東西都在這個資料夾裡。）

*【關係子句裡的動詞，以及縮短】*

## 動詞跟先行詞一致；縮成分詞或不定詞片語

〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉講過：一句話裡有關係子句時，就有兩組主詞與動詞，每個動詞都要跟它所屬子句的主詞一致。例如 `my mother, who has four siblings, loves me`：[出處](https://www.awelu.lu.se/language/common-problems-and-how-to-avoid-them/subject-verb-agreement/agreement-with-the-right-noun-phrase-1/)

*   has 跟 who 一致，who 指的是 my mother，所以用第三人稱單數
*   loves 跟 my mother 一致

所以關係子句裡的動詞，要看 who／which／that 指的是哪個名詞：

*   `Employees who work overtime must record their hours.`（who 指 employees，複數：work）
*   `Each applicant who passes the test will be interviewed.`（who 指 applicant，單數：passes）
*   `The report that was sent to the board contained an error.`（that 指 report，單數：was）
*   `A customer whose orders are late will receive a discount.`（關係子句的主詞是 whose orders，複數：are）

#### 縮短成分詞或不定詞片語

關係子句可以縮成分詞片語或不定詞片語，被修飾的名詞在整句裡當主詞、當受詞都可以：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

*   `The man sitting on the sofa over there is Simon’s brother.`（＝who is sitting）
*   `Don’t forget to fill in the form attached to the letter.`（＝which is attached，the form 是 fill in 的受詞）
*   `The person to ask about going to New Zealand is Beck.`（不定詞片語）

分詞片語怎麼縮，在〈分詞與分詞構句〉那課。

- (C) attending
- (D) attended

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(C)** 整句：參加研討會的人請到櫃檯報到。  
沒有 who，關係子句縮成分詞片語：attending the seminar＝who are attending the seminar（主動用 -ing）。attend、attends、attended 會讓句子出現兩個有時態的動詞卻沒有連接。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** The person ______ about refunds is Ms. Diaz in Customer Service.

- (A) to contact
- (B) contact
- (C) contacts
- (D) will contact

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 整句：退款問題要找的人是客服部的 Diaz 小姐。  
不定詞片語放在名詞後面，意思是「該聯絡的那個人」，跟上面的例句 The person to ask about going to New Zealand is Beck. 同一個寫法。contact、contacts、will contact 會讓句子多出一個有時態的動詞卻沒有連接。

</details>

### 重點整理

*   先行詞是人用 who，是東西用 which，that 兩種都行（只限沒有逗號的子句）；關係代名詞已經是主詞或受詞，子句裡不再加 he、it。
*   看空格後面：直接接動詞＝缺主詞（who、which、that，whom 不行）；接主詞＋動詞而缺受詞＝受詞（who、whom、which、that 都可以）。受詞的關係代名詞在限定子句裡可以省略，主詞的不能省。
*   whose＋名詞＝「……的」，人和東西都能用；空格後面的名詞是先行詞「的」東西、前面沒有 the、a，用 whose；後面是主詞（the、a 開頭的名詞，或 we、Jane 這類）＋動詞而缺受詞，用 who、whom、which、that；who's 是 who is。
*   非限定子句：前後加逗號、不能用 that、不能省略；有逗號是補充，沒逗號是「只有那些」。which 可以指前面整句話（, which surprised everyone）；數量詞＋of whom／of which。
*   介系詞放前面時用 to whom、in which，不用 who、that；where、when、why＝at／in which、on／in which、for which。空格後面是完整的子句用 where、when，缺受詞用 which、that（the hotel where we stayed／the hotel which we recommended）。
*   what＝the thing that，前面沒有先行詞；前面有名詞就用 which、that。關係子句的動詞跟先行詞一致（employees who work、each applicant who passes），whose 子句跟 whose 後面的名詞一致。

## 隨堂測驗

**Q1. The consultant ______ we hired last month has already suggested several changes.**

- (A) who
- (B) which
- (C) whose
- (D) what

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 先行詞 consultant 是人，空格後面是 we hired，缺受詞；受詞位置用 who（也可以用 whom、that）。which 不用在人；whose 後面要接先行詞「的」名詞；what 前面不能有先行詞。

</details>

**Q2. The new copier, ______ was installed on Monday, has already broken down.**

- (A) which
- (B) that
- (C) what
- (D) who

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 有逗號的非限定子句，先行詞是東西、缺主詞，用 which。that 不能用在非限定子句；what 前面不能有先行詞；who 指人。

</details>

**Q3. We need to find a supplier ______ products meet our safety standards.**

- (A) whose
- (B) who
- (C) which
- (D) where

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 空格後面接名詞 products，是「供應商的產品」，用 whose。

</details>

**Q4. This is the office ______ I worked when I first joined the company.**

- (A) where
- (B) which
- (C) that
- (D) what

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** I worked 是完整的子句（work 在這裡不接受詞），指地點用 where（＝in which）。which、that 要在句尾補 in 才對。

</details>

**Q5. Mr. Okada is the person to ______ you should send your application.**

- (A) whom
- (B) who
- (C) that
- (D) which

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 介系詞 to 直接放在前面、指人，用 whom。介系詞後面不直接接 who 或 that；which 不用在人。

</details>

**Q6. ______ surprised us most was the low cost of the project.**

- (A) What
- (B) Which
- (C) That
- (D) It

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 前面沒有先行詞，「最讓我們驚訝的事」＝the thing that surprised us most，用 What。

</details>

**Q7. The engineers who ______ the new bridge will present their plan tomorrow.**

- (A) designed
- (B) designs
- (C) designing
- (D) has designed

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** who 指 engineers（複數）；designs、has designed 是單數的形式，designing 不是有時態的動詞，只有 designed 對。

</details>

**Q8. Sales rose by 20 percent last year, ______ pleased the shareholders.**

- (A) which
- (B) that
- (C) what
- (D) it

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 讓股東高興的是「業績成長兩成」這整件事，指前面整句話用 which。it 會變成兩個完整句子只用逗號接起來。

</details>

**Q9. 哪一句的關係代名詞可以省略？**

- (A) The training course that we attended was very useful.
- (B) The trainer who led the course was very experienced.
- (C) The course, which lasted three days, was very useful.
- (D) The room that was used for the course was too small.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** The training course that we attended… 的 that 是 attended 的受詞（後面接主詞 we），可以省。led、was used 前面的 who、that 是主詞，不能省；有逗號的非限定子句也不能省。

</details>

**Q10. The day ______ the new office opens has not been decided yet.**

- (A) when
- (B) where
- (C) what
- (D) whose

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 先行詞 day 是時間，後面 the new office opens 是完整的子句，用 when。指時間不用 where。

</details>
