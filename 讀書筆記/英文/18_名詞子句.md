*【一整個子句，當一個名詞用】*

## 名詞子句是什麼：四個位置、三種開頭

**子句**（clause）是有自己的主詞和動詞的一組字。主要子句通常由主詞（一個名詞片語）加動詞片語組成。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses)

有些子句不單獨成句，而是整個放進另一個句子裡，站在名詞會站的位置：當主詞、當受詞、當補語，或放在介系詞後面。這種子句叫**名詞子句**（noun clause）。例如 `Whether he wins is not my concern.` 裡，`Whether he wins` 是名詞子句，當整句的主詞。[出處](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/if-and-whether-part-1/5646993.html)

| 位置 | 例句 | 名詞子句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主詞 | `What the client wants is a lower price.`（客戶要的是更低的價格。） | `What the client wants` |
| 受詞 | `We believe (that) the new system will save time.`（我們相信新系統會省時間。） | `(that) the new system will save time` |
| 補語 | `The problem is that the budget is too small.`（問題是預算太少。） | `that the budget is too small` |
| 介系詞後面 | `We talked about whether we should hire a contractor.`（我們討論了要不要找外包。） | `whether we should hire a contractor` |

出處：[劍橋（受詞）](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-that-clause)、[英國文化協會（補語）](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verb-be)、[劍橋（介系詞後面）](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if-or-whether)

名詞子句常見的開頭有三種：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reporting-verbs-wh-if-clauses)

*   that 子句：`He said that I had to see a doctor.`
*   wh- 子句：`She explained what we had to do.`
*   if 子句：`He asked if I was ready.`

三種開頭各說什麼：

*   **that**：說一件事（「……這件事」）。`We believe that the price is fair.`
*   **whether／if**：說「是不是……」。`I don't know whether the price is fair.`
*   **疑問詞**（what、who、where、when、why、how、which）：說「什麼、誰、哪裡……」。`I don't know what the price is.`

> 名詞子句＝一整句話塞進名詞的位置：主詞、受詞、補語、介系詞後面。

## that 子句：動詞、形容詞、名詞後面，以及 It…that

that 當連接詞，把動詞、形容詞或名詞和後面的子句連起來。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/that-clauses) that 在這裡只是連接詞，翻譯時常常不用翻出來。

#### 動詞＋that 子句

常接 that 子句的動詞有兩類：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/that-clauses)

*   **轉述的動詞**：say、tell、admit……
*   **講想法的動詞**：believe、think、know、hope……
*   **其他常見的**：agree、announce、confirm、explain、mention、notice、promise、realise、suggest、understand 等。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-that-clause)

**that 可以省略**：這些動詞後面的 that 常常省略，尤其是非正式的口語，在 guess、think、hope、reckon 後面特別常見。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-that-clause)

*   `He said that I had to see a doctor.` ＝ `He said I had to see a doctor.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reporting-verbs-wh-if-clauses)

**tell、inform 後面要先接「人」：**

*   **先接人再接 that 子句的動詞**：有些轉述動詞可以先接間接受詞（聽的人），再接當直接受詞的 that 子句，例如 advise、assure、convince、inform、persuade、promise、remind、tell、warn。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-patterns-verb-that-clause)
*   **tell**：不能少掉人。✗ `He told that it would take a long time.` → ✓ `He told us that it would take a long time.`
*   **inform**：句型也都先接人，例如 inform somebody that…：`I am pleased to inform you that you have won first prize in this month's competition.`[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/inform)
*   **warn**：清單裡其他動詞不一定要接人，例如 warn 也能直接接 that 子句：`Aid agencies have repeatedly warned that a humanitarian catastrophe is imminent.`[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn)
*   **say**：對照之下，`say` 後面不直接接人，屬於直接接 that 子句的那一組。

#### 形容詞＋that 子句、名詞＋that 子句

*   **be＋形容詞＋that 子句**：表達意見和感受，常見的形容詞有 sure、certain、right、important、afraid、pleased、sorry、surprised、worried。省略 that 意思不變：`I'm sure (that) you'll know a lot of people there.`
*   **名詞＋that 子句**：常見的名詞有 belief、fact、hope、idea、possibility、suggestion、statement、claim：`Dutch police are investigating the possibility that a bomb was planted on the jet.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/that-clauses)

#### that 子句當補語、當主詞

放在 be 後面當補語：有些名詞後面接 be 再接 that 子句，常見的名詞有 answer、argument、belief、claim、explanation、hope、idea、rule、thing。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verb-be)

*   `The problem was that I had no money.`
*   `The obvious explanation is that he simply forgot.`

that 子句也能放句首當主詞，但比較正式；一般會把它移到句尾，前面放一個 `It` 佔住主詞的位置。這個 it 叫「虛主詞」，真正的主詞是後面的子句。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/it)

*   一般：`It's good that she's doing more exercise.`
*   比較正式：`That she's doing more exercise is good.`
*   被動語態也常用：`It was decided that we should all swim across the lake before breakfast.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/it)

> That＋子句＋is＋形容詞（正式）＝ It is＋形容詞＋that＋子句（一般）。

職場例句：

*   `The supplier has confirmed that the parts will arrive on Monday.`（供應商確認零件週一會到。）
*   `I think we should postpone the launch.`（我覺得應該延後上市。）think 後面省略 that。
*   `Ms. Lin told us that the meeting had been moved to Thursday.`（林小姐告訴我們會議改到週四。）
*   `We are pleased that the project finished ahead of schedule.`（很高興專案提前完成。）
*   `There is a possibility that the launch will be delayed.`（上市有可能延後。）
*   `It is clear that the project needs more funding.`（很明顯，這個專案需要更多經費。）
*   `The main concern is that the costs keep rising.`（最大的顧慮是成本一直上升。）

*【是不是，whether 與 if】*

## whether／if 子句：什麼時候只能用 whether

把一個「是不是」的問題（回答 yes 或 no 的問題）放進句子裡，用 `whether` 或 `if` 開頭。間接的是非問句和有 or 的問句用 whether，而且 whether（或 if）不能省略：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whether)

*   ✗ `They asked me I was tired.` → ✓ `They asked me whether (if) I was tired.`

兩個都能用的時候，if 比 whether 常見；比較正式的場合常偏好 whether。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if-or-whether)

#### 只能用 whether 的四種情況

*   **後面直接接 to 不定詞：**✗ `if to buy` → ✓ `I don't know whether to buy the blue one or the red one.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if-or-whether)
*   **介系詞後面：**✗ `interested in if` → ✓ `The police seemed mainly interested in whether there were any locks on the windows.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if-or-whether)
*   **緊接著 or not：**✗ `if or not` → ✓ `Can you tell me whether or not you're interested in the job.` or not 放句尾的話，if 和 whether 都可以：`I called Bill to find out if he really did go to Afghanistan or not.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if-or-whether)
*   **句首當主詞**：if 不當句子主詞的開頭；if 放在句首時是在講條件，不是主詞的一部分。✗ `If he wins is not my concern.` → ✓ `Whether he wins is not my concern.`[出處](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/if-and-whether-part-1/5646993.html)

> to 不定詞前、介系詞後、緊接 or not、句首當主詞 → 只能用 whether。

whether 也能帶出「不管是不是……」的意思（`Whether you like it or not, you're going to have to look after your sister.`）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/whether)；這種 whether 子句不是名詞子句，而是修飾整句話。配對的 whether…or 在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉那課。

職場例句：

*   `Please ask the hotel if the conference room is available on Friday.`（請問飯店週五會議室有沒有空。）
*   `We need to decide whether to renew the contract.`（我們得決定要不要續約。）
*   `The client's decision depends on whether we can lower the price.`（客戶的決定要看我們能不能降價。）
*   `Whether the product will sell well in Europe is still unclear.`（產品在歐洲會不會賣得好還不清楚。）
*   `The committee discussed whether or not the budget should be increased.`（委員會討論了預算該不該增加。）
*   `I'm not sure if the shipment has left the warehouse or not.`（我不確定貨出倉庫了沒。）

*【問句放進句子裡，語序改回直述句】*

## 疑問詞子句（間接問句）：Do you know where the office is?

把一個用 what、who、where、when、why、how 開頭的問句放進另一個句子，就是**間接問句**（indirect question）。重點只有一個：**間接問句的語序跟直述句（一般陳述的句子）一樣，主詞在動詞前面，不倒裝**，也就是把問句的結構（例如 Do you like）改成直述句的結構（例如 I like）：

*   `Where is the bus station?` → `I asked her where the bus station was.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/reported-speech-indirect-speech)
*   `'What time does the train leave?'` → `He asked me what time the train left.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/reported-speech-questions)

#### 改寫三步驟

*   **疑問詞留在最前面**（where、what time、how much 整組一起搬）。
*   **主詞搬回動詞前面**：where is the office → where the office is；when will the parts arrive → when the parts will arrive。
*   **問句裡幫忙的 do／does／did 拿掉**，時態改回動詞身上：how much does it cost → how much it costs；when did it happen → when it happened（例如 When did it happen? → `I asked her when it had happened`）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/reported-speech-indirect-speech)

| 直接問句 | 間接問句（放進句子裡） |
| --- | --- |
| `Where is the meeting room?` | `Could you tell me where the meeting room is?` |
| `How much does the software cost?` | `Do you know how much the software costs?` |
| `When will the parts arrive?` | `I'd like to know when the parts will arrive.` |
| `Why did the order ship late?` | `Please explain why the order shipped late.` |
| `Who approved the budget?` | `I don't know who approved the budget.`（who 本身是主詞，語序不變） |

**疑問詞本身就是主詞時，語序不用改**：who、what 帶出的間接問句裡，疑問詞可以是子句的主詞或受詞，例如 `I asked them who came to meet them at the airport.`（who 是 came 的主詞；原句 Who came to meet you at the airport?）。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/reported-speech-indirect-speech)

**問號跟著整句走：**

*   **單純轉述**：轉述別人問了什麼，句尾不用問號。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/reported-speech-indirect-speech)
*   **整句在問人**：句子本身在問人，就照樣用問號，例如 `Can you tell me where she left the keys?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/where)、`Do you know how I can get to the bus station?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/how)。

職場上用 `Could you tell me…?`、`Do you know…?` 問路、問資訊，就是間接問句最常見的用途。

**疑問詞＋to 不定詞**：講「該怎麼做、該去哪裡」時，疑問詞後面可以直接接 to 不定詞：`I didn't know where to go.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reporting-verbs-wh-if-clauses)、`I can't decide what to buy Liz for her birthday.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/what)。

**when 後面可以用 will：**〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉講過，when 當「在……的時候」時，子句用現在式代替 will；但 when 當疑問詞（「什麼時候」）放進間接問句時，後面可以用 will，例如 `I wonder when the new computers will arrive.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/when) 這課的間接問句都是這種情況。

> 間接問句：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞（不倒裝、不加 do）。Do you know where the office is?

直接問句裡，只有助動詞和情態助動詞能放在主詞前面，一般動詞不行，例如不說 `Where was bought the painting?`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/questions-typical-errors) 間接問句連助動詞都不搬到前面，所以 where is the office、where does the office 這種形狀放進句子裡都是錯的。

*【what 自己就是名詞】*

## what 對 that：what＝the thing(s) that

what 可以當代名詞，意思是「……的東西」（the thing(s) that）：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/what)

*   `What we need to do is make a list of useful phone numbers.`（我們需要做的是……）
*   `I haven't got many Beatles CDs, but you can borrow what I have.`

what 跟 that 最大的差別：**what 自己在子句裡當主詞或受詞**，what we need 的 what 是 need 的受詞，What happened 的 What 是 happened 的主詞；**that 只是連接詞**，自己不在子句裡當任何角色，所以後面跟的是一個完整的句子。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/that-clauses)

| 句子 | 子句裡缺什麼 | 用 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `______ the client needs is a faster delivery.` | needs 少了受詞 | `What` |
| `______ happened at the meeting surprised everyone.` | happened 少了主詞 | `What` |
| `We know ______ the client needs a faster delivery.` | 不缺（the client needs a faster delivery 是完整的句子） | `that` |
| `It is clear ______ the project needs more time.` | 不缺 | `that` |

> 做題時的看法：空格直接接一個子句時，子句缺主詞或受詞 → what；子句是完整的句子 → that。

這是做題時看空格的方法，不是文法書上的規則：what 還有別的用法，例如接名詞的 `what time the train leaves`（what 在這裡修飾 time），這時後面的子句看起來也是完整的。

另外兩個限制：

*   **what 前面不能有先行詞**（被說明的名詞）：what 不當關係代名詞，要用 which。✗ `the book what the lecturer mentioned`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/what) 先行詞後面該用 which、who 還是 that，見〈關係詞〉。
*   **接 that 子句的字後面不能換成 what：**✗ `happy what you can come` → ✓ `I am very happy that you can come and visit us.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/what)

職場例句：

*   `What the client needs is a faster delivery schedule.`（客戶需要的是更快的出貨時程。）
*   `Please read what I sent you yesterday before the meeting.`（開會前請先看我昨天寄給你的東西。）
*   `The new manager explained what we had to do.`（新經理說明了我們要做的事。）
*   `The survey shows that most customers prefer online payment.`（調查顯示大多數客戶偏好線上付款。）
*   `What surprised everyone was the size of the order.`（讓大家驚訝的是訂單的量。）

*【要求、建議，後面用原形】*

## suggest、require、insist、It is important that：that 子句用原形

要求、建議、堅持這一類動詞後面的 that 子句，動詞常用**原形**（字典上的樣子，不加 -s、不改過去式）。有些轉述動詞後面可以接 that＋原形動詞，這部分的動詞是假設語氣（subjunctive），沒有時態：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/patterns-reporting-verbs)

*   `We suggest that she read the documents carefully before signing.`
*   `Steiner demanded that the government investigate the issue.`

注意兩個例子：主詞是 she 卻用 read（不是 reads），主要動詞 demanded 是過去式、子句卻用 investigate（不是 investigated）——這就是「沒有時態」的意思。英文文法把這種原形也歸在假設語氣（subjunctive）底下；用 if 講「如果……就……」的條件句在〈假設語氣〉那課。

哪些動詞：轉述動詞表達「想要某件事發生」時，可以接這種原形，包括 advise、ask、command、demand、insist、order、propose、recommend、request、suggest、urge。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/patterns-reporting-verbs)

require（規定、要求）也一樣：[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/require)

*   `The law requires that everyone wear a seat belt.`
*   `The situation required that he be present.`
*   英式也可以說 `The situation required that he should be present.`

*   **be 動詞用 be，被動用 be＋過去分詞：**`The UN has demanded that all troops be withdrawn.`（所有部隊「被撤出」）。[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/demand_2)

*   **It is important that…也用原形：**`It is important that he attend every day.`[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/important) 同理，essential、necessary、vital 這類「很重要、必要」的形容詞後面的 that 子句也常見原形。

*   **英式英文也用 should＋原形**：原形和 should＋原形兩種都對，英式也可以加 should：[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/important)[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insist)[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/recommend)

    *   `(British English) It is important that he should attend every day.`
    *   `(British English also) He insists that she should come.`
    *   `(British English also) I recommend that he should see a lawyer.`
    *   suggest 是過去式時，that 子句可以用 should：`Her doctor suggested that she should reduce her working hours and take more exercise.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/suggest)

> suggest／recommend／require／request／insist／demand＋that＋主詞＋原形（be＋過去分詞）。

#### 不是「要求」的時候，照一般時態

轉述動詞跟「想要」無關時，後面的 that 子句可以用各種時態，不需要用原形；insist 也可以這樣用。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/patterns-reporting-verbs)

insist 有兩個意思：[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insist)

*   **要求某件事發生**：that 子句用原形（上一段）。
*   **清楚說出某件事是真的，尤其別人不信的時候**：也就是「堅稱」，照一般時態：`The supplier insisted that the goods were in perfect condition when they left the factory.`（供應商堅稱貨出廠時完好無缺。）

#### suggest、recommend 的其他句型

suggest 後面不接「人＋to 不定詞」，也不直接接 to 不定詞，要說：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/suggest)

*   `He suggested that I should apply for a job…`
*   `Dora suggested waiting until the news had been announced officially.`

recommend 也一樣：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/recommend)

*   ✗ recommend to do → ✓ `I recommend taking an umbrella`
*   ✓ `I recommend you take an umbrella`

動詞後面接 -ing 還是 to 不定詞，整理在〈不定詞與動名詞〉。

職場例句：

*   `The manager suggested that Mr. Park call the client before Friday.`（經理建議朴先生週五前打給客戶。）
*   `The client has requested that the delivery date be changed to March 15.`（客戶要求把交貨日改到 3 月 15 日。）
*   `Our lawyer recommended that we review the contract carefully.`（律師建議我們仔細看過合約。）
*   `It is essential that every visitor wear a security badge.`（每位訪客都必須配戴識別證。）
*   `The director insisted that the report be submitted by noon.`（主管堅持報告中午前要交。）
*   `The policy requires that all expenses be approved in advance.`（規定所有費用要事先核准。）

- (A) wore
- (B) wearing

## 綜合練習

做題順序：先看空格後面是不是一個子句；再看那個子句缺不缺主詞或受詞（缺 → what 或其他疑問詞）、是不是「是不是」的問題（→ whether／if）、是不是陳述一件事（→ that）；最後檢查子句的語序是不是直述句。

**🧩 小練習：** Could you tell me ______ the quarterly report is due?

- (A) who
- (B) what
- (C) when
- (D) which

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(C)** 整句：可以告訴我季報什麼時候要交嗎？  
要問的是「什麼時候到期」，用疑問詞 when，後面直述句語序 the quarterly report is due。who、what、which 在這個子句裡都沒有位置：主詞 the quarterly report 已經有了，is due 後面也不缺受詞。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** ______ we need right now is more time to test the software.

- (A) That
- (B) What
- (C) Whether
- (D) If

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(B)** 整句：我們現在需要的是更多時間測試軟體。  
need 少了受詞，What 補上：What we need（我們需要的東西）當主詞。Whether、If 後面要完整的句子，而且 If 不當主詞的開頭。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** The client asked ______ we could deliver the goods by Friday.

- (A) what
- (B) that
- (C) which
- (D) if

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(D)** 整句：客戶問我們能不能在週五前交貨。  
客戶問「能不能週五前交貨」，是非問句放進句子，用 if（或 whether）。what、which 在子句裡沒有位置。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** It is recommended that each passenger ______ at the gate 30 minutes before departure.

- (A) arriving
- (B) arrive
- (C) to arrive
- (D) arrived

**答案：(B)** 整句：建議每位旅客在起飛前 30 分鐘到登機門。  
It is recommended that＋主詞＋原形：arrive（主詞 each passenger 也不加 -s）。arrived 是過去式，跟「建議旅客要怎麼做」對不上；-ing、to 不定詞不能當子句的動詞。

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** I'm not sure who ______ the presentation at tomorrow's meeting.

- (A) will he give
- (B) giving
- (C) will give
- (D) gave

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(C)** 整句：我不確定明天的會議上誰會做簡報。  
who 是主詞（「誰會報告」），語序不變：who will give。will he give 多塞了一個主詞，而且是問句語序；giving 沒有時態；gave 是過去式，跟 tomorrow's meeting 衝突。

**🧩 小練習：** We are interested in ______ your company offers discounts for bulk orders.

- (A) whether
- (B) if
- (C) that
- (D) what

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

### 重點整理

*   名詞子句是一整個子句當名詞用：當主詞、受詞、補語，或放在介系詞後面；開頭是 that、whether／if 或疑問詞。
*   that 子句說一件事；think、say、hope 和 sure、afraid 後面的 that 常省略；tell、inform 後面要先接人；that 子句當主詞時一般改成 `It is…that…`。
*   whether／if 說「是不是」；to 不定詞前、介系詞後、緊接 or not、句首當主詞，只能用 whether。
*   間接問句用直述句語序：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞，拿掉 do／does／did；疑問詞本身是主詞時語序不變；疑問詞＋to 不定詞（how to use）。
*   what＝the thing(s) that，自己在子句裡當主詞或受詞，前面不能有先行詞；that 只是連接詞，後面接完整的句子。
*   suggest、recommend、request、require、insist、demand 表示要求，以及 It is important／essential that，子句用原形（被動用 be＋過去分詞），英式也用 should＋原形；insist 當「堅稱」時照一般時態。
