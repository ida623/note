*【先看懂「倒裝」在做什麼】*

## 倒裝：把助動詞搬到主詞前面

**倒裝**（inversion）就是把句子平常的語序倒過來，最常見的是把主詞和動詞的順序對調。最熟悉的倒裝其實就是問句：

*   `She sings.` → `Does she sing?`
*   `They are working.` → `Are they working?`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/inversion)

這課要講的倒裝，都是**直述句長得像問句**：意思不是在問，只是某個字放到句首，後面的主詞和助動詞就照問句的順序排。做法跟問句一模一樣：

*   句子裡已經有助動詞（have、will、can、should……）或 be 動詞：把它搬到主詞前面。`We have never seen…` → `Never have we seen…`。
*   句子裡沒有助動詞：照問句的規矩補 do／does／did，主要動詞改回原形（現在簡單式補 do／does、過去簡單式補 did）：`We rarely find such talent.` → `Rarely do we find such talent.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials)

> 倒裝＝句首的字＋助動詞（或 be）＋主詞＋其餘部分。沒有助動詞就補 do／does／did，動詞回原形。

這種倒裝用來加強語氣，尤其是在正式的英文裡。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials) 公司公告、正式書信、致詞稿都看得到；平常講話照一般語序就好。

*【Never、Rarely、Not only、Hardly、No sooner、Only after、Under no circumstances】*

## 否定副詞放句首：Never have we…、Not only did…

在正式的文體裡，帶否定意思的副詞（never、seldom、rarely、scarcely、hardly 這類）放在句首來強調時，主詞和助動詞（或情態助動詞）要倒裝：`One seldom hears a politician say ‘sorry’.` → `Seldom does one hear a politician say ‘sorry’.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/inversion)

會引起倒裝的開頭可以分成下面幾組：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials)

| 開頭 | 意思 | 職場例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Never、Rarely、Seldom | 從來沒有、很少 | `Never have we received so many orders in one week.`<br>`Rarely does the CEO attend team meetings.` |
| Not only…but (also) | 不只……還…… | `Not only did the team finish early, but it also stayed under budget.` |
| Hardly／Scarcely…when | 一……就……（過去） | `Hardly had the meeting started when the power went out.` |
| No sooner…than | 一……就……（過去） | `No sooner had we launched the website than orders started coming in.` |
| Only after／Only when／Only then／Only later | 直到……才 | `Only after the audit did we find the error.` |
| Under no circumstances、At no time、In no way、On no account、Not until | 絕不、從來沒有、直到……才 | `Under no circumstances should staff share their passwords.`<br>`Not until Friday did the client reply.` |
| Little | 幾乎不、完全沒 | `Little did we know that the deal would fall through.` |

每一組要注意的地方：

*   **Never、rarely、seldom**：常跟現在完成式、過去完成式，或 can、could 這類情態助動詞一起用，例如 `Seldom has she taken a day off work.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials)
*   **Not only**：為了加強語氣可以放在子句開頭，這時主詞和動詞要倒裝；沒有助動詞或 be 動詞時用 do、does、did：`Not only did she forget my birthday, but she also didn’t even apologise for forgetting it.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/not-only-but-also) 倒裝只發生在 Not only 那一半，but 後面照一般語序。not only…but also 不放句首的用法在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉講過。
*   **Hardly…when、No sooner…than**：都是一件事一發生，另一件馬上接著發生。通常配過去完成式；hardly、scarcely、barely 後面那半句用 when，no sooner 用 than。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials) 例如 `Hardly had I arrived there when I was called back to the head office 100 miles away.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/hardly)、`No sooner had they started their walk than it started to rain.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/no-sooner)。先發生的那件用過去完成式（倒裝在這半句），接著發生的那件用過去簡單式；過去完成式在〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉。
*   **Only＋時間或條件**：only after、only if／when、only then、only later，例如 `Only when they refilled my glass did I realise it was broken.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials) 注意倒裝的位置：Only when 後面那個子句（they refilled my glass）照一般語序，倒裝的是**後面的主要子句**（did I realise）。
*   **no、not 開頭的片語**：under no circumstances、on no account、at no time、in no way、on no condition、not until、not only，例如 `Under no circumstances should children travel without an adult.`[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials)
*   **not＋介系詞片語或子句放句首**：也要倒裝，例如 `Not till I got home did I realise my wallet was missing.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/inversion)
*   **Little**：這種句子裡的 little 是否定或限制的意思，例如 `Little did she know then that she would become the company director.`（＝She didn't know then…）[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-after-negative-adverbials)

**不倒裝的 only**：only 只修飾主詞時，主詞本身就在句首，語序照常，例如 `Only Jason knows where the key is kept.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/only) 所以看到 Only 開頭先分辨：

*   Only 後面直接是主詞：不倒裝，`Only the manager can approve refunds.`
*   Only 後面是時間、條件、副詞：倒裝，`Only after approval`、`Only then`

更多職場例句：

*   `Never has our company faced such strong competition.`（我們公司從沒遇過這麼強的競爭。）
*   `Seldom do our drivers work on weekends.`（我們的司機很少週末上班。）沒有助動詞，補 do，work 用原形。
*   `Not only does the new model use less power, but it is also quieter.`（新機型不只比較省電，也比較安靜。）
*   `Scarcely had the guests arrived when the fire alarm went off.`（客人才剛到，火警警報就響了。）
*   `Only then did the manager realize the mistake.`（經理到那時才發現錯誤。）
*   `At no time was customer data at risk.`（客戶資料從頭到尾都沒有外洩的風險。）be 動詞直接搬到前面。
*   `On no account should this door be left unlocked.`（這扇門絕對不可以不上鎖。）

## Should you…、Were I…、Had we known…

條件句也能拿掉 if、改用倒裝，意思不變、語氣更正式。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-conditionals) 完整的講解與練習在〈假設語氣〉。整理成表：

| 條件句 | if 版 | 倒裝版 | 怎麼改 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 第一類（真的有可能） | `If you have any questions, …` | `Should you have any questions, …` | if 換成 Should，動詞用原形 |
| 第二類（跟現在事實相反，或未來不太可能） | `If I were in your position, …`<br>`If prices rose, …` | `Were I in your position, …`<br>`Were prices to rise, …` | be 動詞直接換成 Were；其他動詞改成 Were＋主詞＋to＋原形 |
| 第三類（跟過去事實相反） | `If we had known, …` | `Had we known, …` | 拿掉 if，Had 搬到主詞前面 |
| 否定 | `If I hadn't been busy, …` | `Had I not been busy, …` | not 放在主詞後面，不寫成 Hadn't I |

出處：[英國文化協會](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/inversion-conditionals)、[劍橋](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/were)

完整例句（Should 開頭這種用在正式的場合）：

*   `Should a customer wish to cancel their booking, they should notify us within seven days.`
*   `Should you wish to cancel your order, please contact our customer service department…`
*   `Had I not been busy this morning, I would have attended the meeting.`

做題時的線索：句首是 Should、Were、Had，而且後面跟著逗號和另一個子句，多半是省略 if 的倒裝。三個字怎麼選，看主詞後面接什麼：原形 → Should；過去分詞 → Had；to 不定詞，或者主詞後面沒有動詞（直接接名詞、形容詞、in your position 這類片語）→ Were。最後再看主要子句是 will（或祈使句）、would＋原形，還是 would have＋過去分詞，對回表中的三類。

## 附和句與放在句首的地點、分詞

#### So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞：「……也是」「……也不」

前面講了一件事，要說「另一個人也一樣」，用 so（肯定）或 neither／nor（否定），後面倒裝：助動詞放前面、主詞放後面，主要動詞不重複：

*   肯定（so）：`Geoff is a very good long-distance runner and so is his wife.`、`They all joined the new gym and after three weeks so did he.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/so)
*   否定（neither、nor）：`Neither can I.`、`Nor does Gina.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/neither-neither-nor-and-not-either)
*   ✗ `I can’t also.` → ✓ `Neither can I.`

助動詞要跟前面那句對上：前面是 be 就用 be、是 can 就用 can、前面是一般動詞的過去式就用 did、現在完成式就用 has／have。不倒裝的說法是 `not … either`，意思一樣是「也不」，但助動詞和主詞的語序不變：`I haven’t either.`（或 `Neither have I.`／`Nor have I.`）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/neither-neither-nor-and-not-either) nor 接在否定句後面連接兩個子句、後面要倒裝，在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉講過（`We didn’t see the factory, nor did we meet the manager.`）。

*   `Our team met its sales target, and so did the Osaka team.`（我們團隊達標了，大阪團隊也是。）met 是過去式 → did。
*   `Ms. Ho is attending the trade fair, and so is her manager.`（何小姐會去參展，她的經理也會。）
*   `The printer on the third floor isn't working, and neither is the copier.`（三樓的印表機壞了，影印機也壞了。）
*   `We haven't received the samples. — Neither have we.`（我們還沒收到樣品。——我們也還沒。）
*   `I can't join the call at 3 p.m. — I can't either.`（我三點不能參加通話。——我也不能。）不倒裝的說法。

#### Here comes…、There is…：地點副詞放句首

here 與 there（當地點副詞時）放句首可以倒裝，而且可以直接用主要動詞，不需要助動詞：`Here comes the bus!`、`Here’s your coffee.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/inversion) 但主詞是代名詞時不倒裝：✗ `There is it` → ✓ `There it is, on the sink.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/here-and-there)

*   `Here comes the airport shuttle.`（機場接駁車來了。）主詞是名詞，倒裝。
*   `Here it comes.`（它來了。）主詞是代名詞 it，不倒裝。
*   `Here are the reports you asked for.`（這是你要的報告。）主詞 the reports 是複數，用 are。

#### Attached is…、Enclosed please find…：書信裡把分詞放句首

書信裡常把 attached（附在電子郵件裡的）、enclosed（隨信裝在信封裡的）放到句首，後面接 be 動詞再接主詞，例如 `Attached is the agenda for Monday's meeting.`（附件是週一會議的議程。）它就是 `The agenda for Monday's meeting is attached.` 把 attached 搬到前面，**主詞還是後面的 the agenda**，所以 be 動詞要跟後面的名詞一致：

*   主詞複數用 are：`Attached are the minutes and the updated schedule.`
*   同樣的語序：`Enclosed herewith is a letter for him.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/herewith)
*   另一種常見的寫法是 `Please find enclosed…`／`Please find attached…`，例如 `Please find enclosed an application form and information about the company.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/enclose) 〈分詞與分詞構句〉的信件用語也整理過。

> So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞（助動詞跟前一句對上）；Here comes＋名詞，但 Here it comes；Attached is／are＋主詞（be 動詞跟後面的主詞一致）。

## 省略：意思很清楚時，重複的字可以拿掉

**省略**（ellipsis）是把照文法規矩本來應該出現、但意思已經很清楚的字拿掉。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/ellipsis) 口語裡也常省略（`[Are] You ready?`）；下面是書面、職場文件裡常見的幾種。

#### 副詞子句省略主詞（和 be）：When asked、Once approved、Although expensive

when、while、once、after、before、if、although 帶的子句，主詞跟主要子句相同時，可以把主詞（和 be 動詞）拿掉，只留 -ing、過去分詞或形容詞：

*   **時間連接詞**（when、after、before、until、since、while、once、as、as soon as）：很多後面可以接 -ing 或 -ed 形式，代替主詞＋動詞。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/while)
*   **after、although、though、if**：主詞跟主要子句相同時，後面常接這種沒有時態的子句，例如 `The proposal, if accepted by Parliament, will mean fundamental changes to the education system.`、`By the end of the day, although exhausted, Mark did not feel quite as tired as he had in the past.`（＝although he was exhausted）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

職場例句：

*   `When asked about the delay, the spokesperson declined to comment.`（被問到延誤時，發言人不予置評。）＝When she was asked…，被動留過去分詞。
*   `Once approved, the budget will be sent to the finance department.`（預算一經核准，就會送到財務部。）＝Once it is approved…。
*   `If accepted, the offer will take effect on May 1.`（如果接受，報價從 5 月 1 日生效。）
*   `While waiting for the client, Ms. Kao reviewed the contract.`（等客戶的時候，高小姐把合約看了一遍。）主動留 -ing。
*   `Although more expensive, the new printer is much faster.`（新印表機雖然比較貴，但快很多。）同一個形狀：`Though more expensive, the new model is safer and more efficient.`。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/although-or-though)
*   `While in Singapore, Mr. Park visited two of our suppliers.`（在新加坡的時候，朴先生拜訪了兩家供應商。）同理，while he was in 拿掉 he was，留下介系詞片語。

前提是兩個子句的主詞要一樣：拿掉的主詞會被當成主要子句的主詞。這個「主詞相同」的要求針對的是 When asked、While waiting 這類留下分詞或形容詞的省略；下面的 if necessary、if possible、as requested、unless otherwise stated 是固定說法，不受這條限制。主詞不同還硬省略，就是〈分詞與分詞構句〉講過的懸垂分詞。主動用 -ing、被動用過去分詞的判斷，也跟那課一樣。

**只拿掉主詞、留下有時態的動詞，不行**：and、but、or 後面可以省掉重複的主詞（下面會講），但這種省略在 before、after、because、when、while 這些連接詞後面不適用：[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/ellipsis)

*   ✗ `We watch TV after have dinner.` → ✓ `We watch TV after we have dinner.`
*   要省就改成沒有時態的 -ing：`after having dinner`、`I had something to eat before leaving.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/clauses-finite-and-non-finite)

上面 When asked、While waiting 是沒有時態的子句（留下的是分詞或形容詞，不是有時態的動詞），when、while、after 這類連接詞後面可以這樣用，跟 and、but、or 那種省略是不同的結構。

#### if 後面的固定省略：If necessary、If possible、If so、If not

if 後面有時候可以省略一些字，形成固定說法；意思很明顯時，if 後面直接用 so 或 not：[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/if)

*   `Check the temperature of your meat with a meat thermometer if possible.`（＝if it’s possible）
*   `If not, please email the office.`

職場例句：

*   `If necessary, we can change the delivery date.`（必要的話，可以改交貨日。）
*   `Please reply by Friday if possible.`（可以的話請在週五前回覆。）
*   `Have you received the invoice? If so, please confirm the amount. If not, let me know.`（收到發票了嗎？收到的話請確認金額；沒收到請告訴我。）

#### and、but、or 後面省略重複的主詞

and、but、or 後面不必重複主詞；重複的主詞配 be 動詞時，主詞和 be 都可以省。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/ellipsis) 少掉的字讀的人自己補得回來。[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/ellipsis)

*   `Ms. Lai reviewed the proposal and approved it the same day.`（賴小姐看完提案、當天就核准了。）and 後面省略 she。
*   `The new system is faster and easier to use.`（新系統比較快、也比較好用。）省略 it is。
*   `She can speak French, and her manager Spanish.`（她會講法文，她的經理會講西班牙文。）同理，後半省略重複的 can speak，正式書面才這樣寫，讀到時知道少了什麼就好。

#### to 代替整個不定詞：I'd like to

前面講過的動作，後面不想重複，常常可以只留 to，例如 `Some people give up sugar completely, but I didn't want to [give up sugar completely].`。[出處](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/ellipsis)

*   `Would you like to join us for lunch? — I'd love to.`（要一起吃午餐嗎？——好啊。）
*   `I haven't finished the report yet, but I plan to this afternoon.`（報告還沒寫完，但我打算今天下午寫完。）
*   `You don't have to attend, but you're welcome to.`（你不一定要參加，但歡迎你來。）

#### 比較句的省略：than expected、than usual、as planned

than 後面的子句通常是省略過的子句，例如 `The journey took longer than expected.`（＝than people expected it to take）[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-clauses)

辭典例句：

*   `If all goes ahead as planned, the new superstore will open in November.`
*   `Installing the new computer system has taken far longer than planned.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/plan)
*   `There was more rainfall than usual this summer in the mountain areas.`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/usual)

職場例句：

*   `Sales were higher than expected in the third quarter.`（第三季的業績比預期高。）
*   `The renovation was completed as planned.`（整修照計畫完成了。）
*   `The office will close earlier than usual on Friday.`（辦公室週五會比平常早關。）

注意 than 和 then：比較用 than（than expected），then 是「然後、那時」。比較級的句型在〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉。

#### 文件裡常見的省略片語：few, if any；unless otherwise stated；as requested

*   **little／few, if any**：就算有也很少。這裡的 if 用來講數量或程度，例如 `This time of year we get little, if any, rain (= almost none).`[出處](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/if) 職場例句：`Few, if any, of the applicants had experience with the software.`（應徵者裡就算有人用過這套軟體，也沒幾個。）
*   **unless otherwise stated**：除非另有說明，省略的是 it is，例如 `Unless otherwise stated, all translations in the text are my own.`[出處](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/otherwise) 同理的寫法還有 unless otherwise noted、unless otherwise specified。
*   **as requested、as needed、as agreed**：跟 as planned 同一種形狀（as＋過去分詞），省略的是主詞和 be，例如 `As requested, I have attached the price list.`（依您的要求，附上價目表。）、`Replace the filter as needed.`（視需要更換濾網。）。

> 省略的前提：意思不會變、讀的人知道少了什麼。副詞子句省主詞（When asked、While waiting 這類）要「主詞相同」並改成 -ing、過去分詞或形容詞；不能只拿掉主詞而留下有時態的動詞。if necessary、as requested 這類固定說法不受「主詞相同」限制。

*【整套文法怎麼用在考場】*

## 多益文法題的做題順序：先看選項，再看空格前後

先看考試本身。閱讀測驗共 100 題，讀各種文字材料，自己控制速度作答，分成三個部分：[出處](https://www.ets.org/toeic/about/listening-reading.html)

*   **第五部分句子填空**（Incomplete Sentences）：30 題，每句少了一個字或片語，下面有四個選項。
*   **第六部分段落填空**（Text Completion）：16 題，讀一篇文章，有些句子少了一個字或片語，每格四個選項，選最適合的把文章補完整。考生手冊的範例是一封電子郵件，裡面四個空格，其中一格的四個選項是完整的句子。[出處](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toeic/toeic-listening-reading-test-examinee-handbook.pdf)
*   **第七部分閱讀理解**。

下面是**解題建議**，不是考試規則。

#### 四個步驟

1.  **先看四個選項，不急著讀整句**。四個選項長什麼樣子，大致就決定這題考什麼（下表）。同字根不同詞性、同一個動詞不同時態、連接詞和介系詞混在一起，各有各的看法。
2.  **照那一型，到空格前後找線索**。詞性題看空格前後一兩個字；時態題找時間詞（yesterday、since、by next year）；連接詞題看空格後面接子句還是名詞；關係詞題看先行詞是人還是東西、後面缺主詞還是缺受詞。
3.  **把選的答案放回句子讀一遍**。檢查主詞和動詞單複數一致、主動還是被動、句子是不是只有一個有時態的主要動詞。
4.  **四個選項意思都不一樣、文法都對時，是字彙題**。這時才需要讀懂整句意思，看搭配（apply for、comply with）和上下文。

第六部分的建議：做法一樣，但線索可能不在同一句——時態要對上整封信講的時間、代名詞要對上前文指的人、however／therefore 這類連接副詞要看前後兩句意思是相反還是因果；選整句的那一格，要讀前後文看哪一句接得上。

#### 看到這種選項，想到哪一課

| 四個選項長這樣 → 回去複習 | 先看哪裡 |
| --- | --- |
| 同字根不同詞性：`success／successful／successfully／succeed`<br>→ 〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉 | 空格前後：冠詞、所有格、介系詞後面填名詞；冠詞和名詞之間填形容詞；be 後面填形容詞；修飾動詞或形容詞填副詞 |
| -ing 對 -ed 形容詞：`satisfying／satisfied`、`attached／attaching`<br>→ 〈分詞與分詞構句〉 | 名詞自己做還是被做；說東西還是說人的感受 |
| 名詞單複數、冠詞、數量詞：`information／informations`、`much／many／each／a few`<br>→ 〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉 | 名詞可不可數、單數還是複數 |
| 代名詞的格：`he／him／his／himself`<br>→ 〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉 | 空格在動詞前（主格）、動詞或介系詞後（受格）、名詞前（所有格） |
| 同一個動詞不同時態：`signed／has signed／will sign／signs`<br>→ 〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉〈進行式三態〉〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉；單複數看〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉 | 時間線索（ago、since、by the time、next week），再看主詞單複數 |
| 主動被動混在一起：`approved／was approved／approving／has approved`<br>→ 〈被動語態〉 | 主詞是做事的還是被做的；空格後面有沒有受詞 |
| 情態助動詞：`must／don't have to／should／might`，或助動詞後面的動詞形式<br>→ 〈助動詞〉 | 意思是義務、不必、建議還是推測；助動詞後面接原形 |
| `to do／doing／do／done`<br>→ 〈不定詞與動名詞〉 | 前面的動詞要接 to 還是 -ing；前面是不是介系詞（含 look forward to） |
| 形容詞與副詞的比較：`more／most／-er／as`、`much／very`<br>→ 〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉 | 後面有沒有 than、前面有沒有 the、比較的是兩個還是一群 |
| 介系詞：`at／on／in／by／until／for／during`<br>→ 〈介系詞〉 | 後面是時刻、日期還是一段時間；是期限還是一直持續 |
| 連接詞與介系詞混在一起：`although／despite／because／because of`<br>→ 〈連接詞與連接副詞〉〈介系詞〉 | 空格後面是子句（有主詞＋有時態的動詞）還是名詞 |
| 連接副詞：`however／therefore／moreover／otherwise`<br>→ 〈連接詞與連接副詞〉 | 前後兩句意思是相反、因果還是補充；標點（句號、分號後面接） |
| 關係詞：`who／which／whose／where`<br>→ 〈關係詞〉 | 先行詞是人、東西還是地點；後面直接接動詞＝缺主詞，用 who、which、that；後面是主詞＋動詞而缺受詞，用 who、whom、which、that；後面的名詞是先行詞「的」東西、前面沒有 the、a，用 whose；後面是完整的句子用 where、when |
| `what／that／whether／if` 開頭的子句，或疑問詞後面的語序<br>→ 〈名詞子句〉〈關係詞〉 | 前面有沒有先行詞；子句當主詞或受詞；間接問句用直述句語序 |
| `would／would have／had／were` 和 if 子句的時態<br>→ 〈假設語氣〉 | if 子句和主要子句的時態要成對；有沒有跟事實相反 |
| 句首是 `Never／Not only／Only after／Should／Had`，選項是同幾個字不同語序<br>→ 本課（倒裝） | 句首的字會不會引起倒裝；倒裝時有沒有補 do／did |
| 四個意思不同的單字：`confirm／delay／cancel／reserve`<br>→ 字彙題，不是文法題 | 整句意思與搭配 |

表裡「先看哪裡」那一欄是每課的判斷法縮成一句；例外與細節要回到那一課看，例如副詞也能放在句尾、連接詞後面也可能接 -ing。

**答案：(B)** 整句：可以告訴我會議室在哪裡嗎？  
問句放進句子裡變成間接問句，用直述句語序：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞，where the meeting room is。where is the meeting room 是直接問句的語序；where does the meeting room 用了問句的 does，而且少了動詞；that the meeting room is 沒有說是「在哪裡」，句子不完整。（〈名詞子句〉）

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** ______ the new policy will reduce costs is still unclear.

- (A) Whether
- (B) If
- (C) What
- (D) Which

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 整句：新政策會不會降低成本，目前還不清楚。  
「新政策會不會降低成本」是一個是非問題，放在句首當主詞，只能用 Whether，不能用 If。What、Which 後面的子句要缺東西，這裡 the new policy will reduce costs 是完整的句子。（〈名詞子句〉）

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** If we ______ the order yesterday instead of today, it would have arrived by now.

- (A) placed
- (B) have placed
- (C) would place
- (D) had placed

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(D)** 整句：如果我們昨天就下單、而不是今天才下，貨現在應該已經到了。  
主要子句是 would have arrived，講跟過去事實相反（昨天其實沒下單，今天才下），if 子句用 had＋過去分詞：had placed。placed 會變成第二類的形式，對不上 would have；have placed、would place 不放在這種 if 子句。（〈假設語氣〉）

</details>

**🧩 小練習：** If the shipment ______ late, our team will contact you right away.

- (A) arrives
- (B) will arrive
- (C) arrived
- (D) would arrive

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 整句：如果貨晚到，我們團隊會馬上跟您聯絡。  
主要子句是 will contact，講未來真的可能發生的事，if 子句用現在式 arrives，不用 will。arrived、would arrive 是跟事實相反的形式，對不上 will。（〈假設語氣〉）

</details>

### 重點整理

*   倒裝＝句首的字＋助動詞（或 be）＋主詞，語序跟問句一樣；沒有助動詞就補 do／does／did，動詞回原形。用在正式的書面與致詞。
*   否定副詞放句首要倒裝：Never、Rarely、Seldom、Little、Not only（倒裝只在這一半）、Under no circumstances、At no time、Not until。Hardly／Scarcely…when、No sooner…than 通常配過去完成式。Only after／when／then 倒裝的是後面的主要子句；Only 只修飾主詞（Only the manager can…）不倒裝。
*   省略 if 的條件句倒裝：Should＋主詞＋原形（第一類）、Were＋主詞（＋to 原形）（第二類）、Had＋主詞＋過去分詞（第三類）；否定 not 放主詞後面（Had I not）。完整講法在〈假設語氣〉。
*   附和句：So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞，助動詞跟前一句對上（met → did、hasn't → has）；不倒裝的說法是 not … either。Here comes＋名詞，但代名詞不倒裝（Here it is）；Attached is／are 的 be 動詞跟後面的主詞一致。
*   省略：副詞子句主詞相同時可以省主詞和 be（When asked、Once approved、Although expensive、While in…）；if necessary、as requested 這類固定說法不受主詞相同的限制；不能只拿掉主詞而留下有時態的動詞（after having lunch，不是 after have lunch）。if necessary／if possible／if so／if not；than expected、than usual、as planned；I'd like to；few, if any；unless otherwise stated。
*   做題順序（建議）：先看四個選項是哪一型 → 到空格前後找那一型的線索 → 放回句子檢查單複數、主被動、只有一個主要動詞 → 選項意思都不同才是字彙題。第六部分的線索可能在前後句。

## 隨堂測驗

**Q1. Never ______ such a large order from a single client.**

- (A) have we received
- (B) we have received
- (C) we have never received
- (D) have we receive

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** Never 放句首要倒裝：助動詞 have 搬到主詞 we 前面，後面接過去分詞 received。we have received 沒倒裝；we have never received 跟句首的 Never 重複否定；have 後面要過去分詞。

</details>

**Q2. ______ you require further assistance, please contact the front desk.**

- (A) Should
- (B) Would
- (C) Were
- (D) Had

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 省略 if 的倒裝：Should you require…＝If you require…，後面是原形 require、主要子句是祈使句。Would 開頭會變成問句；Were 後面要接 to 不定詞；Had 後面要過去分詞。

</details>

**Q3. Had the shipment ______ on time, we would have met the deadline.**

- (A) arrived
- (B) arrive
- (C) arrives
- (D) arriving

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** Had＋主詞＋過去分詞＝If the shipment had arrived，跟過去事實相反，配主要子句 would have met。had 後面要過去分詞，arrive 是原形、arrives 是現在式、arriving 是 -ing，都接不上 had。

</details>

**Q4. Our team didn't receive the memo, and neither ______ the sales department.**

- (A) did
- (B) was
- (C) has
- (D) didn't

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 前一句是過去簡單式的否定 didn't receive，附和「也沒有」用 neither＋did＋主詞。was、has 對不上前一句的助動詞；neither 已經是否定，不再加 didn't。

</details>

**Q5. Only after the contract is signed ______ the project.**

- (A) can we start
- (B) we can start
- (C) we start
- (D) can we started

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** Only after 帶的子句（the contract is signed）照一般語序，倒裝的是後面的主要子句：can we start。can 後面接原形，不接 started。

</details>

**Q6. When ______ about the budget cuts, the director gave a clear answer.**

- (A) asked
- (B) asking
- (C) ask
- (D) was asked

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 董事是「被問」的，原句 When he was asked…，主詞相同，省略主詞和 was 留下過去分詞 asked。asking 是主動；was asked 前面少了主詞，不能只拿掉主詞留下有時態的動詞。

</details>

**Q7. The renovation took much longer ______ expected.**

- (A) than
- (B) then
- (C) as
- (D) that

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** longer 是比較級，配 than：than expected（比預期的久）。then 是「然後」；as 要配 as…as；that 不接在比較級後面。

</details>

**Q8. ______ the delay, the conference started on time.**

- (A) Despite
- (B) Although
- (C) Because
- (D) Unless

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 空格後面是名詞 the delay，用介系詞 Despite。Although、Because、Unless 是連接詞，後面要接子句。這是「先看空格後面接子句還是名詞」的那一型。

</details>

**Q9. The supplier ______ products we ordered last month has gone out of business.**

- (A) whose
- (B) who
- (C) which
- (D) what

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** 空格後面的名詞 products 是供應商「的」產品、前面沒有 the、a，用 whose：the supplier whose products we ordered。who、which 後面要接缺主詞或缺受詞的子句，這句 products we ordered 前面已經有完整的名詞，對不上；what 前面不能有先行詞。

</details>

**Q10. Unless otherwise ______, all meetings will be held in Room B.**

- (A) noted
- (B) noting
- (C) note
- (D) notes

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案：(A)** unless otherwise noted＝unless it is otherwise noted（除非另有說明），是「被說明」，省略主詞和 be 後留過去分詞 noted。noting 是主動；note、notes 是有時態的動詞卻少了主詞。

</details>
